Session 1 - Inventory Management

Item Lookup and Stock Balance — MMS060 and MMS200

45 min
Objective

Check the on-hand stock balance for an item in a specific warehouse in M3.

Scenario

A customer asks if you have stock of item ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE) available in the Carrollton warehouse. Look up the balance in MMS200.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MMS200 and filter by warehouse
  1. Open MMS200 (Item. Open Toolbox) from the left sidebar search.
  2. In the Filter Options, set Warehouse = CAR (Carrollton).
  3. In the Item number filter field, type ARM51830031.
  4. Click Apply to filter the list.
2. View the stock balance
  1. ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE) appears in the list with item type VCT and status 20.
  2. Right-click the row and select Related > Balance Identity to open MWS068.
  3. MWS068 shows the on-hand balance by location - note Location = CAR1A10 and On-hand approve = 24.
  4. This tells you there are 24 units on hand at location CAR1A10 in the CAR warehouse.
3. Interpret the balance
  1. On-hand approve = 24 means 24 units are approved and available.
  2. The Lot number field shows A1234 - this is the lot the stock is assigned to.
  3. If the quantity is sufficient for the customer order, proceed to enter the order in OIS300.
  4. If stock is low, check Availability All Warehouses (Related > Availability All Warehouses) to see other locations.
Expected Outcomes
  • Stock balance checked in MMS200 for item ARM51830031.
  • Location CAR1A10 confirmed with 24 units on hand.
  • Availability confirmed before entering customer order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can look up any item in MMS060 and read its status, UOM, and category
  • I can navigate to MMS080 to view warehouse-specific parameters like reorder point
  • I can check stock balance in MMS200 and distinguish on-hand, allocated, and available
  • I always use available quantity (not on-hand) when committing stock to customers
Knowledge Check
1. Item master records are managed in:
  • OIS100
  • CRS610
  • MMS060 (correct)
  • PPS200
2. When promising stock availability to a customer, you should quote:
  • The on-hand quantity
  • The allocated quantity
  • The available quantity (on-hand minus allocated) (correct)
  • The reorder point quantity
3. To transact against an item, the item status must be:
  • Discontinued (50)
  • Pending (10)
  • Active (20) (correct)
  • On hold (30)
Session 2 - Inventory Management

Transfers and Adjustments — MMS100 and MMS150

60 min
Objective

Transfer inventory from one warehouse location to another in M3.

Scenario

Stock of item ARM51830031 needs to be moved from location CAR1A10 to another location in the CAR warehouse. Perform the transfer in MMS200.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MMS200 and locate the item
  1. Open MMS200 (Item. Open Toolbox) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR and Item number = ARM51830031, then click Apply.
  3. Right-click the ARM51830031 row and select Related > Balance Identity to confirm current location CAR1A10 with 24 on hand.
2. Initiate the warehouse transfer
  1. Right-click the ARM51830031 row in MMS200 and select Related > Stock Transaction History or look for a Move/Transfer option.
  2. Alternatively, open MMS060 (Stock Balance. Open) directly from the sidebar.
  3. In MMS060, filter for item ARM51830031 in warehouse CAR.
  4. Right-click the balance line at location CAR1A10 and look for the Move or Transfer option.
3. Enter transfer details and confirm
  1. Enter the To location (destination location in CAR warehouse).
  2. Enter the quantity to transfer.
  3. Confirm lot number A1234 carries over to the new location.
  4. Submit the transfer - M3 updates the balance at both locations.
  5. Return to MWS068 Balance Identity to verify the stock moved to the new location.
Expected Outcomes
  • Stock transfer initiated for ARM51830031 in CAR warehouse.
  • Quantity moved from location CAR1A10 to destination location.
  • Balance confirmed at new location in MWS068.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open MMS100 and enter a valid FROM and TO location transfer
  • I verify available quantity in MMS200 before entering a transfer
  • I can confirm the transfer and verify both location balances updated in MMS200
  • I log inventory discrepancies rather than covering them with transfers
Knowledge Check
1. MMS100 is used to:
  • Adjust inventory quantities up or down
  • Move inventory from one location to another (correct)
  • Process a cycle count
  • Create a new item in the item master
2. Before entering a transfer quantity, you should verify in MMS200 that:
  • The item is on a customer order
  • The available quantity at the FROM location is sufficient (correct)
  • The item has a supplier price agreement
  • The TO location bin is empty
3. If MMS200 shows stock at a location you cannot physically find, you should:
  • Transfer it to a different location to clear the balance
  • Log the discrepancy in the issue log — do NOT cover it with a transfer (correct)
  • Delete the inventory record
  • Call IT to reset the balance
Session 3 - Inventory Management

Cycle Counting — MMS300

60 min
Objective

Perform a physical inventory count and enter the count result in M3.

Scenario

A cycle count is being performed at CAR warehouse, location CAR1A10. The physical count for item ARM51830031 shows 22 units. Enter the count in M3 and review the variance.

Practice Tasks
1. Check the current book balance
  1. Open MMS200, set Warehouse = CAR, filter Item number = ARM51830031, click Apply.
  2. Right-click ARM51830031 and select Related > Balance Identity (MWS068).
  3. Confirm current On-hand approve = 24 at location CAR1A10, lot A1234.
  4. Note this as your book quantity before the count.
2. Enter the physical count
  1. Open MMS165 (Physical Inventory. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Search for an open count for warehouse CAR, location CAR1A10.
  3. Locate item ARM51830031 in the count list.
  4. Enter the counted quantity = 22.
  5. Save the count entry.
3. Review the variance and approve
  1. M3 calculates the variance: Book = 24, Count = 22, Variance = -2.
  2. Review the variance - a negative variance means the physical count is lower than the book.
  3. If the variance is within tolerance, approve the count.
  4. M3 adjusts the on-hand balance to 22 at location CAR1A10.
  5. Return to MWS068 to confirm the updated balance.
Expected Outcomes
  • Physical count entered in MMS165 for ARM51830031 at CAR1A10.
  • Variance of -2 reviewed (book 24 vs count 22).
  • Balance adjusted to 22 after count approval.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open MMS150 and enter an inventory adjustment with item, location, and quantity
  • I select the correct reason code for each type of adjustment
  • I enter a clear explanatory note on every adjustment (required for audit)
  • I get manager approval before entering adjustments above $500
  • I notify Finance same-day for any inventory write-off
Knowledge Check
1. To remove damaged goods from inventory, use adjustment reason code:
  • CYCLE
  • RCVERR
  • DAMAGE (correct)
  • WRITUP
2. In MMS150, to ADD stock you enter:
  • A negative quantity
  • Zero
  • A positive quantity (correct)
  • The difference from the last count
3. Inventory adjustments above $500 in value require:
  • IT department approval
  • Manager approval BEFORE entering the adjustment (correct)
  • CFO sign-off within 30 days
  • Finance to enter them in GLS100 instead
Session 4 - Inventory Management

AAA - Automatic Allocation Algorithm

45 min
Objective

Review item warehouse settings and update replenishment parameters in M3.

Scenario

The buyer wants to review and update the reorder point and order quantity for item ARM51830031 in the CAR warehouse. Update the settings in MMS200.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the item warehouse record in MMS200
  1. Open MMS200 (Item. Open Toolbox) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR, filter Item number = ARM51830031, click Apply.
  3. Click Change (or double-click) on the ARM51830031 row to open the item/warehouse detail.
2. Review replenishment settings
  1. Navigate to the replenishment or planning panel (look for panel tabs at the bottom).
  2. Review the Reorder point - this is the stock level that triggers a new purchase order.
  3. Review the Order quantity - this is the standard quantity ordered when replenishment triggers.
  4. Note the Safety stock value if populated.
3. Update the parameters and save
  1. Update the Reorder point to the new value provided by the buyer.
  2. Update the Order quantity if required.
  3. Click Next or Save to store the changes.
  4. Confirm the updated values are saved by reopening the record.
  5. The buyer can run a material plan to verify the new parameters take effect in MRP.
Expected Outcomes
  • Item warehouse record opened in MMS200 for ARM51830031 in CAR.
  • Replenishment parameters (reorder point, order quantity) reviewed.
  • Updated values saved to the item/warehouse record.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open MMS300 and navigate a cycle count list
  • I enter physical count quantities accurately — zero for items not found
  • I review all variances before posting and recount large discrepancies
  • I understand the manager must approve before M3 posts the adjustments
Knowledge Check
1. If an item on the cycle count list is not found at the listed location, you enter:
  • The last known quantity
  • The system balance as the count
  • Zero (correct)
  • Skip the item
2. For a large variance found during a cycle count, you should:
  • Post it immediately to correct the system
  • Conduct a physical recount before posting (correct)
  • Contact the supplier to resolve it
  • Transfer stock from another location to balance it
3. Cycle count adjustments are posted after:
  • Any team member confirms the count
  • Manager review and approval (correct)
  • IT validates the count list
  • Finance approves all variances
