Session 1 - Customer Service and Order Entry

Manual Order Entry — Standard Net Terms (CL1)

60 min
Objective

Enter a standard customer sales order in M3 using order type C01.

Scenario

Customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) calls in an order for 5 CT of item ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE). Enter the order.

Practice Tasks
1. Open OIS100 and find the customer
  1. Press Ctrl+S to open Search and Start. Type OIS100, select Customer Order. Open from the list, and click OK.
  2. The Account Search box pops up. Type 000382 and press Tab (you can also search by name, city, or phone). Click the customer.
  3. Once the customer is found, the header fills in automatically: Customer, Payer, CO type C01, Req delivery dt, and Facility 101. Review it.
  4. Need a different delivery date? Click the calendar icon. The Delivery calendar shows the customer's route, delivery days, and the 3:00 PM cutoff the day before. Pick a delivery day and click Close.
  5. Click Next.
2. Fill in Order Details (OIS100/I)
  1. Check the defaults: Warehouse CAR, Priority 5-Norm, Payment terms N30, Delivery method OT (Our Truck).
  2. In Customer's ord, enter the customer's PO number. This field is required.
  3. Click Next.
3. Enter the order line (OIS101)
  1. Use the entry row under the grid. Whs defaults to CAR, Ln tp stays 0, and Req dt carries over from the header.
  2. Enter Item ARM51830031. Don't know the item number? Open Item search, type part of the number or name, and click Search. Check the CAR column for stock (green means available), then click Pick stock.
  3. Enter Order qty 5 and confirm U/M = CT. Sales price fills in from the customer price list.
  4. Click Add. The line saves right away. There is no separate Save button. The line drops into the grid and the Running Total updates.
  5. Note the CO no (order number) for follow-up.
4. Verify the order in OIS300
  1. Press Ctrl+S to open Search and Start. Type OIS300, select it from the list, and click OK.
  2. In the Customer filter, type 000382 and press Enter.
  3. Find your CO no. Lst (lowest status) and Hi (highest status) should both read 33-ALLOCATED. That means the stock is reserved and the order is ready for the warehouse.
  4. If your order shows 20-Line Not Entered, the header was saved with no lines. Click the CO no to open it and add the lines, or tell your trainer so it can be deleted.
  5. Next stop is the warehouse. A 33-ALLOCATED order sits in MWS410 (Delivery. Open Toolbox) showing 0-Release until its pick list is released. Once released, OIS300 shows 44-PICK RELEASED.
Expected Outcomes
  • Sales order created in M3 with CO type C01.
  • Customer PO entered in Customer's ord.
  • Order line entered with correct item, quantity, and price.
  • Order shows 33-ALLOCATED in OIS300 and is waiting in MWS410 for pick release.
  • CO number noted for follow-up.
Exception Handling
Alert: Customer's order number must be entered
Click OK, enter the customer's PO in Customer's ord on the Order Details screen, then click Next.
OIS300 shows 20-Line Not Entered
The order header exists but no lines were added. Open the CO and add the lines, or have your trainer delete the empty order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I open programs with Ctrl+S (Search and Start)
  • I can start an order in OIS100 from Account Search and check the header defaults
  • I always fill in Customer's ord (the customer PO)
  • I can add lines in OIS101 and know they save when I click Add
  • I can find my order in OIS300 and read 33-ALLOCATED vs 20-Line Not Entered
Knowledge Check
1. When you enter a customer number in OIS100, M3 automatically fills in:
  • Item prices only
  • Name, address, payment terms, and price list (correct)
  • Warehouse location and carrier
  • Nothing — you fill all fields manually
2. The customer PO number field is important because:
  • It is required to release the order
  • It prints on the invoice and packing slip for customer reference (correct)
  • It auto-populates the price list
  • It routes the order to the correct warehouse
3. A new order shows 33-ALLOCATED in OIS300. That means:
  • The order has shipped
  • Stock is reserved and the order is waiting for pick release in MWS410 (correct)
  • The order is on credit hold
  • The header saved without lines

Session 3 - Customer Service and Order Entry

Order Entry — When an Order Is Held for Credit

60 min
Objective

Understand what happens when a customer order is stopped due to a credit limit in M3.

Scenario

You enter an order for a customer who has exceeded their credit limit. M3 places a CO stop on the order. Learn what the stop looks like and who to contact.

Practice Tasks
1. Enter the order
  1. Press Ctrl+S to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and click + (New). OIS100/A opens.
  2. Enter Customer = 000382, CO type = C01.
  3. Enter Req delivery dt and click Next.
  4. Add an order line in OIS101 and click Add.
2. Identify the credit stop
  1. Return to OIS100/I (Generic Panel) for the order.
  2. Look at the CO stop field - a value of 3 means Credit limit stop.
  3. A CO stop prevents the order from advancing to picking and shipping.
  4. Do NOT release the stop without authorization from AR.
3. Escalate to AR
  1. Contact the AR team with the customer number, CO no, and order amount.
  2. AR will review the account and either release the stop or ask the customer to pay.
  3. Once released, the CO stop field will clear and the order can proceed.
  4. Add a note in Your ref 1 with the date and who approved the release.
Expected Outcomes
  • Credit stop identified on the order (CO stop = 3).
  • AR team contacted with order details.
  • Process for release understood.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can recognize a credit stop on an order
  • I can read (not change) CRS610 panel J and K
  • I escalate to credit and never work around a hold
  • I know a prepay hold shows as C10 on the delivery, not as a credit flag
  • I document who I called and when
Knowledge Check
1. Who releases a credit-held order?
  • The CSR who entered it
  • Credit / AR, in OIS120 (correct)
  • The warehouse
  • IT
2. Where are a customer's credit limits and payment terms?
  • OIS101
  • CRS610 panel J (correct)
  • MMS001
  • PPS200
3. A prepay order isn't moving and OIS300 shows no credit flag. Where do you look?
  • CRS620
  • MWS410, for a C10 stop on the delivery (correct)
  • GLS200
  • OIS010

Changing an Order After It's Entered

45 min
Objective

Change quantity, delivery method, and ship-to on an order that's already entered.

Scenario

Customer 000300 (A TO Z CARPET CENTER) has an open OT order with two lines. They call back: make line 1 eight cartons, they'll pick it up instead (WC), and the rest goes to a jobsite.

Practice Tasks
1. Change the line quantity
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS300, and click OK. Filter Customer = 000300 and find the order.
  2. Click the order row and click Order lines in the right panel.
  3. Click line 1, then click Change (the pencil) in the toolbar.
  4. In Order qty alt, type 8 and click Next. The line saves right away and shows 8 in the grid.
2. Switch OT to WC
  1. Press F3 to go back to OIS100 with the CO no filled in, then click Change (the pencil).
  2. Click Next to Payment and Conditions. Change Delivery method to WC and click Next.
  3. M3 asks whether to change all lines. Click Yes.
  4. Click through to the end. Check that Delivery method shows WC WILL CALL.
3. Send it to a one-time jobsite
  1. Back in OIS300, click the order row and click Addresses in the right panel.
  2. Check Chg adr and click Next.
  3. Clear Address no and press Enter. Click OK on Confirm. The name and address lines turn white.
  4. Type the jobsite name and street.
  5. Click Next. When M3 asks to change all lines, click Yes.
  6. The Delivery Address on OIS102 now shows the jobsite.
Expected Outcomes
  • Line 1 shows 8 CT.
  • Delivery method is WC on every line.
  • The order ships to the jobsite. The customer's saved address is unchanged.
Exception Handling
M3 asks about route retrieval when I change the delivery method
That's normal. M3 recalculates the route, so the delivery date can move. Check Cfdldt on the lines afterward.
The delivery fee is still on the order after switching to WC
It doesn't come off by itself. Waive it in Charges (ord-11).
I need to change the order to a drop ship or the ship-from warehouse
This isn't set up yet. Ask your lead.

  • I can change a line quantity in OIS101
  • I can switch OT to WC or CC and know what the Yes/No prompt does
  • I check charges after changing the delivery method
  • I can put a one-time jobsite address on one order
Knowledge Check
1. You change a line quantity in OIS101 and click Next. What else do you need to do to save it?
  • Click Save in the toolbar
  • Nothing. It saves when you click Next (correct)
  • Run OIS300
  • Ask the warehouse
2. You switch an order from OT to WC and M3 asks about all lines. Yes means:
  • Only the first line changes
  • Every line on the order changes to WC (correct)
  • The order is cancelled
  • The customer record changes
3. Where do you type a one-time jobsite address for one order?

Objective

Scenario
