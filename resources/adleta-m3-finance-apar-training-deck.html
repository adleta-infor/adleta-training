Session 1 - Finance — Accounts Payable

Supplier Invoice Entry with PO Match — APS100

60 min
Objective

Enter an AP invoice in M3 and match it to the corresponding purchase order receipt.

Scenario

An invoice arrives from supplier S900400 (AHF PRODUCTS). Enter the invoice in APS100 and match it to the PO receipt.

Practice Tasks
1. Open APS100 and select the FAM function
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS100, and open Supplier Invoice. Record.
  2. A division selection screen appears - select division AAA (Adleta 100/AAA).
  3. Select FAM Function 300 (Approved NMS=3 - PO, D/Note, Suppl O/No) and click Apply.
  4. Click + (New) to enter a new invoice.
2. Enter the invoice header
  1. Enter Supplier = S900400.
  2. Enter the Supplier invoice no from the paper invoice.
  3. Enter Invoice date and the invoice amount.
  4. Confirm Currency = USD.
  5. Click Next.
3. Match to the PO and post
  1. On the invoice line panel, enter the PO number from the invoice (format: 20010XXXXX).
  2. M3 retrieves the receipt lines - confirm quantity and price match the invoice.
  3. If the amounts match, approve the invoice.
  4. If there is a price discrepancy, place the invoice on hold (see fin-4).
Expected Outcomes
  • AP invoice entered in APS100 against supplier S900400.
  • Invoice matched to PO receipt.
  • Invoice approved or placed on hold if discrepancy found.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open APS100 in division AAA with FAM 300
  • I enter the supplier invoice number and date exactly
  • I can match to the PO and compare to the receipt
  • I hold mismatches instead of approving them
Knowledge Check
1. Which FAM function is used for PO-matched invoices in the exercise?
  • 101
  • 201
  • 300 (correct)
  • 900
2. The invoice date you enter is:
  • Today
  • The date on the supplier invoice (correct)
  • The PO date
  • The receipt date
3. The invoice price doesn't match the PO. You:
  • Approve it
  • Hold it and work it with Purchasing (correct)
  • Change the PO yourself
  • Delete the invoice
Session 2 - Finance — Accounts Payable

Payment Proposal — Check Run (CH1)

60 min
Objective

Create an AP payment proposal and process a check run for supplier invoices.

Scenario

Supplier S900400 (AHF PRODUCTS) has invoices due. Create a payment proposal in APS130 and process the check run.

Practice Tasks
1. Open APS130 and create a payment proposal
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS130, and open Suppl Payment Proposal. Open.
  2. Click + (New) to create a new proposal.
  3. Set the payment method and due date range to capture invoices due for S900400.
  4. Run the proposal - M3 selects eligible invoices.
  5. Review the selected invoices and remove any that are on hold or in dispute.
2. Approve and process the check run
  1. Confirm the total payment amount on the proposal.
  2. Approve the proposal.
  3. Run the check print process - M3 generates the checks.
  4. Verify checks printed correctly and note the check numbers.
3. Post the payments
  1. Post the payment proposal - M3 clears the AP invoices and records the payments.
  2. Confirm the invoices no longer appear in open AP.
  3. File the printed checks for mailing.
Expected Outcomes
  • Payment proposal created in APS130 for supplier S900400.
  • Check run processed and checks printed.
  • AP invoices cleared after posting.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I check batches are posted and disputes held before the run
  • I can create and review a CH1 proposal
  • I get approval before printing and posting
  • I confirm the invoices cleared in APS200
Knowledge Check
1. Removing an invoice from a payment proposal:
  • Voids it
  • Leaves it open for the next run (correct)
  • Deletes the supplier
  • Pays it twice
2. Removing an invoice from a payment proposal in APS200:
  • Voids the invoice permanently
  • Leaves it open for future payment runs (correct)
  • Sends a rejection notice to the supplier
  • Creates a credit memo automatically
3. Once payments are processed in APS200 they:
  • Can be reversed with one click
  • Cannot be reversed in M3 — errors require manual journal entries (correct)
  • Are automatically emailed to suppliers
  • Are sent to Birst for reporting

Holding a Supplier Invoice

1 hour
Objective

Place an AP invoice on hold to prevent it from being included in payment runs.

Scenario

An invoice from supplier S900400 has a price discrepancy. Hold the invoice in APS450 so it is excluded from payment proposals until resolved.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the invoice in APS450
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS450, and open Supplier Invoice Batch. Open.
  2. Set Division = AAA and click Apply.
  3. Search for invoices from supplier S900400.
  4. Locate the invoice with the price discrepancy.
2. Place the invoice on hold
  1. Right-click the invoice and look for a Hold or Change Status option.
  2. Enter a hold reason - example: Price mismatch, PO $2.50 vs Invoice $2.75.
  3. Save the hold status.
  4. Confirm the invoice is excluded from the next payment proposal in APS130.
3. Notify and resolve
  1. Contact supplier S900400 to resolve the price discrepancy.
  2. Once resolved, return to APS450 and release the hold.
  3. The invoice will be included in the next payment proposal run.
Expected Outcomes
  • Invoice from S900400 placed on hold in APS450.
  • Hold reason documented.
  • Invoice excluded from payment runs until resolved.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I know when to hold an invoice
  • I can find it in APS450 / APS200
  • I enter a clear hold reason
  • I release the hold when resolved and note why
Knowledge Check
1. A held invoice:
  • Is paid next run
  • Stays out of payment runs until released (correct)
  • Is deleted
  • Goes to the GL
2. Every hold needs:
  • A clear reason (correct)
  • A new PO
  • A customer number
  • Nothing
3. The supplier's full ledger is in:
  • APS200 (correct)
  • OIS350
  • GLS990
  • CRS610
4. How often should held invoices be reviewed?
  • Weekly (correct)
  • Yearly
  • Never
  • Only at audit

AP Payment Proposal — Wire Transfer (WID)

1 hour
Objective

Create a wire transfer payment proposal for a supplier requiring electronic payment.

Scenario

A supplier requires payment by wire transfer instead of check. Create the payment proposal in APS130 and process the wire.

Practice Tasks
1. Create the wire payment proposal in APS130
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS130, and open Suppl Payment Proposal. Open.
  2. Click + (New) to create a new proposal.
  3. Set Payment method = WID (wire transfer) instead of CH1.
  4. Select invoices for the supplier due for payment and run the proposal.
  5. Confirm bank account details for the supplier and verify the total wire amount.
2. Approve and submit the wire
  1. Review and approve the wire proposal.
  2. Submit the wire file to the bank through your banking portal.
  3. Note the wire reference number from the bank.
3. Post the wire payment
  1. Post the payment proposal in APS130 - M3 clears the AP invoices.
  2. Confirm the invoices no longer appear in open AP.
  3. Record the bank wire reference number in M3 for audit purposes.
Expected Outcomes
  • Wire transfer proposal created in APS130 with payment method WID.
  • Wire submitted to bank.
  • AP invoices cleared after posting.

  • I understand that confirming in M3 does not initiate the actual bank transfer
  • I can review and confirm a payment proposal before processing

2. Before running a wire payment proposal, invoices must be:
  • On hold

3. After confirming the payment proposal in M3, what additional step is required?
  • Nothing — the wire is sent automatically
  • Coordinate with Finance to initiate the actual bank transfer (correct)
  • Void the invoices
  • Print a check
4. Wire payment proposals are run from:
  • PPS200

Voiding an AP Check

1 hour
Objective

Void an AP check that was issued in error or cannot be mailed.

Scenario

A check printed to a supplier was damaged and cannot be mailed. Void the check in M3 and reopen the invoices for re-payment.

Practice Tasks
1. Locate the check in APS130
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS130, and open Suppl Payment Proposal. Open.
  2. Search for the payment proposal that generated the check.
  3. Locate the specific check by supplier name or check number.
  4. Confirm the check has not already been cashed.
2. Void the check
  1. Select the check and look for the Void option under Actions or right-click menu.
  2. Enter the void reason (example: Check damaged - reissue required).
  3. Enter the void date.
  4. M3 reopens the associated invoices back to open AP status.
3. Verify and re-queue for payment
  1. Confirm the invoices are back in open AP in APS450.
  2. Confirm the voided check is no longer in open payments.
  3. Create a new payment proposal in APS130 to reissue the payment.
Expected Outcomes
  • Check voided in APS130.
  • Associated invoices reopened to open AP.
  • New payment proposal created to reissue the check.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I stop payment at the bank before voiding a lost check
  • I can void a check with my trainer
  • I confirm invoices reopened in APS200
Knowledge Check
1. For a lost check, the first step is:
  • Void in M3
  • Call the bank to stop payment (correct)
  • Reissue
  • Nothing
2. After a void, the invoices:
  • Are deleted
  • Go back to open (correct)
  • Are paid
  • Go to AR
3. Confirm the invoices reopened in:
  • APS200 (correct)
  • OIS300
  • MWS410
  • GLS040

Recoding an Invoice and Reading the Supplier Ledger — APS110, APS200

45 min
Objective

Correct the GL coding on a posted supplier invoice.

Scenario

An AHF PRODUCTS (S900400) freight invoice was coded to the wrong cost center.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the invoice
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS200, and click OK.
  2. Find supplier S900400 and the freight invoice. Note the invoice number and current coding.
2. Recode
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type APS110, and click OK.
  2. Find the invoice and enter the correct account string your trainer gives you.
  3. Save and note the reason.
3. Check
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type GLS210, and click OK.
  2. Look up both accounts and find the recode entries.
Expected Outcomes
  • Invoice found in APS200.
  • Coding corrected in APS110.
  • Correction visible in GLS210.
