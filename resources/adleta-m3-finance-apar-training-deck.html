Session 1 - Finance — AP and AR

AP Invoice Entry with PO Match — APS100

60 min
Objective

Enter an AP invoice in M3 and match it to the corresponding purchase order receipt.

Scenario

An invoice arrives from supplier S900400 (AHF PRODUCTS). Enter the invoice in APS100 and match it to the PO receipt.

Practice Tasks
1. Open APS100 and select the FAM function
  1. Open APS100 (Supplier Invoice. Record) from the left sidebar.
  2. A division selection screen appears - select division AAA (Adleta 100/AAA).
  3. Select FAM Function 300 (Approved NMS=3 - PO, D/Note, Suppl O/No) and click Apply.
  4. Click + (New) to enter a new invoice.
2. Enter the invoice header
  1. Enter Supplier = S900400.
  2. Enter the Supplier invoice no from the paper invoice.
  3. Enter Invoice date and the invoice amount.
  4. Confirm Currency = USD.
  5. Click Next.
3. Match to the PO and post
  1. On the invoice line panel, enter the PO number from the invoice (format: 20010XXXXX).
  2. M3 retrieves the receipt lines - confirm quantity and price match the invoice.
  3. If the amounts match, approve the invoice.
  4. If there is a price discrepancy, place the invoice on hold (see fin-4).
Expected Outcomes
  • AP invoice entered in APS100 against supplier S900400.
  • Invoice matched to PO receipt.
  • Invoice approved or placed on hold if discrepancy found.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can enter a supplier invoice in APS100 with correct supplier, number, DATE (from invoice), and amount
  • I can enter the PO reference and run the three-way match using F14
  • I know what a clean match looks like and what happens next (queued for payment)
  • I know NOT to approve a price variance without Purchasing or supplier resolution
  • I log all match discrepancies in the issue tracker
Knowledge Check
1. The Invoice Date field in APS100 should contain:
  • Today's date
  • The date you entered the invoice in M3
  • The date shown on the supplier's invoice (correct)
  • The PO creation date
2. To run the three-way match in APS100 you press:
  • F3
  • F5
  • F14 (correct)
  • F17
3. When a price variance is flagged in APS100 you should:
  • Approve it to keep payments on schedule
  • Contact Purchasing or the supplier to resolve — never approve without authorization (correct)
  • Delete the invoice and re-enter it
  • Change the PO price yourself to match the invoice

Review and update AR settings for a customer including payment terms and credit limit in CRS610.

Review the AR configuration for customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) - confirm payment terms, credit limit, and payment method are set correctly.

Session 4 - Finance — AP and AR

Holding an AP Invoice — APS205

1 hour
Objective

Place an AP invoice on hold to prevent it from being included in payment runs.

Scenario

An invoice from supplier S900400 has a price discrepancy. Hold the invoice in APS450 so it is excluded from payment proposals until resolved.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the invoice in APS450
  1. Open APS450 (Supplier Invoice Batch. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Division = AAA and click Apply.
  3. Search for invoices from supplier S900400.
  4. Locate the invoice with the price discrepancy.
2. Place the invoice on hold
  1. Right-click the invoice and look for a Hold or Change Status option.
  2. Enter a hold reason - example: Price mismatch, PO $2.50 vs Invoice $2.75.
  3. Save the hold status.
  4. Confirm the invoice is excluded from the next payment proposal in APS130.
3. Notify and resolve
  1. Contact supplier S900400 to resolve the price discrepancy.
  2. Once resolved, return to APS450 and release the hold.
  3. The invoice will be included in the next payment proposal run.
Expected Outcomes
  • Invoice from S900400 placed on hold in APS450.
  • Hold reason documented.
  • Invoice excluded from payment runs until resolved.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can place an invoice on hold using APS205
  • I can release an invoice hold after resolution
  • I can reverse a posted invoice in APS200
  • I understand the GL impact of a reversed invoice
  • I know when to hold vs. reverse an invoice
Knowledge Check
1. Invoice holds are managed in which M3 program?
  • APS100
  • APS200
  • APS205 (correct)
  • PPS200
2. After reversing an invoice, M3 automatically:
  • Pays the supplier
  • Creates an offsetting GL entry (correct)
  • Cancels the related PO
  • Emails the supplier
3. A common reason to hold an invoice is:
  • The supplier is a new account
  • A price or quantity discrepancy requiring supplier clarification (correct)
  • The warehouse is busy
  • The PO was cancelled
4. After resolving a hold, you must ___ before payment can process:
  • Create a new invoice
  • Delete the hold record
  • Release the hold in APS205 (correct)
  • Re-enter the PO

Session 6 - Finance — AP and AR

Voiding an AP Check

1 hour
Objective

Void an AP check that was issued in error or cannot be mailed.

Scenario

A check printed to a supplier was damaged and cannot be mailed. Void the check in M3 and reopen the invoices for re-payment.

Practice Tasks
1. Locate the check in APS130
  1. Open APS130 (Suppl Payment Proposal. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Search for the payment proposal that generated the check.
  3. Locate the specific check by supplier name or check number.
  4. Confirm the check has not already been cashed.
2. Void the check
  1. Select the check and look for the Void option under Actions or right-click menu.
  2. Enter the void reason (example: Check damaged - reissue required).
  3. Enter the void date.
  4. M3 reopens the associated invoices back to open AP status.
3. Verify and re-queue for payment
  1. Confirm the invoices are back in open AP in APS450.
  2. Confirm the voided check is no longer in open payments.
  3. Create a new payment proposal in APS130 to reissue the payment.
Expected Outcomes
  • Check voided in APS130.
  • Associated invoices reopened to open AP.
  • New payment proposal created to reissue the check.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can look up a check by supplier or check number in APS220
  • I can void a check with the correct void date and reason code
  • I can verify invoices reopen in APS205 after the void
  • I know to call Fifth Third Bank for a stop payment BEFORE voiding a lost check in M3
Knowledge Check
1. After voiding a check in APS220, the invoices it covered are:
  • Permanently closed — cannot be paid again
  • Open again and available for the next payment run (correct)
  • Sent to the supplier as a credit note
  • Written off to the GL expense account
2. To look up a check in payment history you use:
  • APS100
  • APS200
  • APS205
  • APS220 (correct)
3. For a lost check, BEFORE voiding in M3 you must:
  • Email the supplier to reissue
  • Call Fifth Third Bank to issue a stop payment (correct)
  • Create a new check in APS200 first
  • Post a journal entry in GLS100
Session 7 - Finance — AP and AR

Lockbox and AP Check Printing

35 min
Objective

Understand the daily AP payment and AR cash application process in M3.

Scenario

Learn the end-to-end flow: AP invoices are entered and batched, payment proposals are created in APS130, and customer payments are applied to open AR invoices.

Practice Tasks
1. AP daily process - invoice batching
  1. AP invoices are entered in APS100 (division AAA, function 300) throughout the day.
  2. Batches are reviewed and approved in APS450 (Supplier Invoice Batch. Open).
  3. Invoices on hold or with discrepancies are resolved before the payment run.
  4. Payment proposals are created in APS130 for invoices due.
2. AR daily process - cash application
  1. Customer payments arrive by check, wire, or ACH.
  2. Payments are applied to open AR invoices in M3 using the appropriate AR cash application program.
  3. Unapplied payments are researched and matched to the correct customer and invoice.
  4. Confirm open AR is updated and customer balances are current.
3. End of day verification
  1. Confirm all AP batches for the day are posted.
  2. Confirm all customer payments received are applied in AR.
  3. Run a quick AR aging report to verify no unexpected balances.
  4. Flag any items that need follow-up to the AR or AP team.
Expected Outcomes
  • AP daily batch process understood (APS100 -> APS450 -> APS130).
  • AR cash application process understood.
  • End of day verification steps completed.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can import the daily lockbox file in ARS110 and select the correct bank account
  • I can distinguish auto-matched items (no action) from unmatched items (need manual application)
  • I can manually apply an unmatched payment to the correct open invoice
  • I can post the lockbox batch and verify invoices closed in ARS120
Knowledge Check
1. The lockbox file imported in ARS110 is provided by:
  • M3 nightly processing
  • Fifth Third Bank each business day (correct)
  • The customer by email attachment
  • Birst reporting system
2. Auto-matched lockbox items require:
  • Manual review and approval for each one
  • No action — M3 matched them correctly (correct)
  • Finance director sign-off
  • An additional journal entry in GLS100
3. After posting the lockbox batch, you verify invoices closed in:
  • APS130
  • PPS200
  • ARS120 (correct)
  • GLS200
