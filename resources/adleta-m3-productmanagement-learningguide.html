Session 1 - Product Management

Discontinuing an Item — Status 50 and MRP Off

60 min
Objective

Discontinue an item in M3 by updating its status and reviewing remaining inventory.

Scenario

A flooring style is being discontinued. Update the item status in MMS001 and review remaining stock in the CAR warehouse before the item is phased out.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the item and update status in MMS001
  1. Open MMS001 (Item. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. In the Item number filter, type the item number to discontinue and click Apply.
  3. Click Change (or right-click > Change) on the item row to open the edit screen.
  4. On the Basic Information panel, change Item status from 20-Released to 90-Discontinued (or the appropriate phase-out status).
  5. Save the change.
2. Review remaining inventory in MMS200
  1. Open MMS200 (Item. Open Toolbox), set Warehouse = CAR, enter the item number, click Apply.
  2. Right-click the item and select Related > Balance Identity (MWS068) to see on-hand quantity.
  3. If stock remains, coordinate with purchasing to cancel any open purchase orders for this item.
  4. Notify the sales team so no new customer orders are placed for the discontinued item.
3. Confirm item is no longer orderable
  1. Return to MMS001 and confirm the item status is updated.
  2. Attempt to add the item to a test order in OIS300 - M3 should warn or block the item based on its status.
  3. If the item appears on any active open orders, review those orders with the sales team.
  4. Document the discontinuation date and notify the product manager (Lauren Calvey).
Expected Outcomes
  • Item status updated to discontinued in MMS001.
  • Remaining inventory reviewed in MMS200/MWS068 for CAR warehouse.
  • Sales and purchasing teams notified of discontinuation.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can look up an item in MMS060 and review its status and open orders
  • I can turn off the MRP planning flag and explain what it does
  • I can change Item Status to 50 and verify new orders are blocked in OIS100
  • I add a note to the item with discontinuation date, reason, and authorizer
Knowledge Check
1. Item master records are managed in:
  • OIS100
  • CRS610
  • MMS060 (correct)
  • PPS200
2. Setting the MRP flag to OFF on an item stops M3 from:
  • Shipping the item
  • Auto-generating planned purchase orders for that item (correct)
  • Displaying the item in OIS100
  • Deleting the item
3. Item Status 50 prevents:
  • Receiving against open POs
  • New customer orders from being placed (correct)
  • Existing open orders from shipping
  • The item from appearing in Birst
Session 2 - Product Management

Product Lifecycle and Pricing Structure — MMS060 and OIS010

60 min
Objective

Review an item record in MMS001 and update the customer price list in OIS010.

Scenario

A product manager needs to review the item master for a VCT tile and confirm its pricing in OIS010 for customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES).

Practice Tasks
1. Review the item in MMS001
  1. Open MMS001 (Item. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Filter by Item type = VCT (Vinyl Composition Tile) and Status = 20-Released, then click Apply.
  3. Locate item ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE) in the list.
  4. Click Change to open the item and review: Description, Item type (VCT), U/M (CT), Item mgr (MPARSONS), and P grp (AHC).
  5. Confirm Status = 20-Released and the item is active.
2. Review the price list in OIS010
  1. Open OIS010 (Price List. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Search for the price list that applies to customer 000382 - this is typically LP1.
  3. Filter for item ARM51830031 to see the current price.
  4. Review the price, effective date, and currency (USD).
  5. If the price needs to be updated, click Change and enter the new price and effective date.
3. Verify pricing on a test order
  1. Open OIS300 and create a test order for customer 000382 with item ARM51830031.
  2. Confirm the Net price field on OIS101 populates automatically from price list LP1.
  3. Verify the price matches what was set in OIS010.
  4. Cancel the test order - do not submit it.
Expected Outcomes
  • Item ARM51830031 reviewed in MMS001 - status 20-Released, type VCT.
  • Price confirmed in OIS010 for price list LP1.
  • Price verified on test order in OIS300 for customer 000382.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I understand the product lifecycle stages and how item status controls M3 transactions
  • I know the steps to safely discontinue a product — checking POs, orders, stock, and notifying departments
  • I can add and update item prices in OIS010 with correct effective and expiry dates
  • I verify price changes on a test sales order before communicating to the business
  • I document every price change with effective date and reason and notify affected teams in advance
Knowledge Check
1. To discontinue an item in M3, you set its status to 90. Before doing this you must confirm:
  • The item has been in the system for at least one year
  • There are no open POs, no open sales orders, and zero stock on hand — and you have notified Purchasing, Sales, and Warehouse (correct)
  • Finance has approved the removal
  • IT has deleted the item from the price list
2. Item prices in M3 are stored in:
  • MMS060 on the item record
  • OIS010 on the price list (correct)
  • MMS200 in the stock balance
  • GLS040 in the chart of accounts
3. If a sales order is pulling the wrong price for a customer, the first place to check is:
  • The item record in MMS060
  • The customer's price list assignment in OIS017 (correct)
  • The GL account
  • The purchase order
4. When making a price list change that affects active customers, you should:
  • Make the change immediately and notify no one
  • Update the expiry date but leave the price alone
  • Notify KRISTENB and Sales before updating, document the effective date and reason (correct)
  • Wait until the end of the financial year
Session 3 - Product Management

Master Data Quality and New Product Launch Coordination

60 min
Objective

Complete all master data steps in M3 to set up a new product for purchasing and sales.

Scenario

A new flooring product is being added to the Adleta catalog. Set up the item master in MMS001, assign it to the CAR warehouse in MMS002, and add it to the price list in OIS010.

Practice Tasks
1. Create the item master in MMS001
  1. Open MMS001 (Item. Open) and click + (New) to create a new item.
  2. Enter the Item number (follow Adleta naming convention, e.g., manufacturer prefix + style code).
  3. Enter the item Name and Description 2 (full product description).
  4. Set Item type = VCT (or RES for residential, depending on the product category).
  5. Set U/M = CT (carton) or LF (linear foot) as appropriate.
  6. Set Status = 20-Released and assign Item mgr = MPARSONS (or the responsible product manager).
  7. Set P grp (Product group) = AHC (or appropriate group) and save.
2. Assign the item to the CAR warehouse in MMS002
  1. Open MMS002 (Item. Connect Warehouse) from the left sidebar.
  2. Enter the new item number and Warehouse = CAR.
  3. Set the reorder point, order quantity, and lead time based on purchasing input.
  4. Set Lot control = 3-In lot master if the item is lot-tracked.
  5. Save the item/warehouse record - the item is now available for ordering in the CAR warehouse.
3. Add the item to the price list in OIS010 and test
  1. Open OIS010 (Price List. Open) and locate price list LP1.
  2. Click + (New) to add a line for the new item.
  3. Enter the item number, sales price, and effective date.
  4. Save the price list entry.
  5. Create a test order in OIS300 for customer 000382 - add the new item and confirm the price defaults from LP1.
  6. Cancel the test order after verifying pricing.
Expected Outcomes
  • New item created in MMS001 with correct type, U/M, and status 20-Released.
  • Item connected to CAR warehouse in MMS002 with replenishment parameters.
  • Item added to price list LP1 in OIS010 and verified on test order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I understand my role as master data owner and how item data quality affects every department
  • I can run a regular item master audit and identify items needing attention in MMS060
  • I know the five-step new product launch checklist before changing status to Active
  • I document all item changes with who, when, and why — in MMS060 notes and SharePoint
  • I know how to handle item substitutes and communicate them to Sales with lead time differences
  • I manage seasonal item prices with correct effective and expiry dates in OIS010
Knowledge Check
1. Before a new item's status is changed to Active (20) in MMS060, which five things must be complete?
  • Item number, color, weight, size, and brand
  • Item created, UOM confirmed, price set, Purchasing and Warehouse notified, Sales notified of availability (correct)
  • IT approval, Finance approval, manager sign-off, legal review, and supplier contract
  • Item created, test order placed, shipped, invoiced, and paid
2. If you find a potential duplicate item in MMS060, you should:
  • Immediately inactivate the newer one
  • Coordinate with IT and Purchasing to confirm which record is correct before taking action (correct)
  • Delete both and start over
  • Leave it — duplicates are normal in M3
3. Item substitutes for out-of-stock products should be:
  • Handled verbally between Sales reps without any documentation
  • Documented in the item record notes and shared with Sales, including lead time and availability differences (correct)
  • Created as new item records in MMS060
  • Entered as returns in OIS390
4. A seasonal item whose price expires at end of season should be handled by:
  • Deleting the price record after the season
  • Setting an expiry date on the price in OIS010 when the price is created — not managing it manually after the fact (correct)
  • Leaving the old price active until the next season starts
  • Contacting Finance to remove the price
