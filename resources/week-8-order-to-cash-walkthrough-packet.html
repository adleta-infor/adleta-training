Session 1 - End-to-End Process Walkthroughs

Procure to Pay — Full Walkthrough

90 min

Practice this session using the live portal steps below.

Practice Tasks
1. Verify supplier — CRS620

Purchasing opens CRS620 and confirms the supplier is active, has payment terms, and is ready for a PO.

2. Create PO header — PPS200

Purchasing opens PPS200, clicks New for a new PO. Enters supplier, PO type, buyer, warehouse, and requested delivery date.

3. Add PO lines — PPS201

Purchasing adds at least two lines: item number, quantity, UOM, price, and delivery date. Records the PO number assigned by M3.

4. Approve and release PO

Purchasing confirms the PO status is released/ready per Adleta rules before communicating to the supplier.

5. Receive goods — PPS300

Warehouse opens PPS300, finds the PO, verifies packing slip against PO items and quantities, enters actual received quantities, and confirms. Records receipt number.

6. Verify inventory update — MMS200

Warehouse opens MMS200 and confirms the received item shows updated on-hand and available balances.

7. Enter supplier invoice — APS100

Finance AP opens APS100, enters the supplier invoice referencing the PO number. Enters invoice date, amount, and runs F14 three-way match.

8. Resolve match result

If matched: invoice is auto-approved. If variance: Finance AP contacts Purchasing (price issue) or Warehouse (quantity issue) to resolve before payment.

9. Create payment proposal — APS200

Finance AP opens APS200, includes the approved invoice in a payment proposal. Reviews before processing.

10. Debrief — handoff questions

Group discusses: what must each department get right for the next step to work? Where do delays or errors originate?

Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can trace all 10 steps of the Procure to Pay process
  • I understand what each department must get right before the handoff
  • I can identify whether a match exception is a price variance or quantity variance
  • I know who to contact to resolve each type of exception
  • I completed a proof-of-completion entry with supplier, PO, receipt, and invoice references
Knowledge Check
1. Before Warehouse can receive goods, Purchasing must ensure:
  • The invoice is already entered in APS100
  • The PO is released and the supplier, item, quantity, and UOM are correct (correct)
  • The payment proposal is approved
  • Inventory is at zero for that item
2. The three-way match in APS100 compares:
  • PO, receipt, and supplier invoice (correct)
  • PO, customer order, and payment
  • Supplier invoice, GL account, and payment
  • PO, warehouse, and freight invoice
3. A quantity variance on the three-way match means:
  • The supplier invoice price is wrong
  • The invoice quantity does not match what was received (correct)
  • The PO was not released
  • Finance entered the wrong supplier number
4. Which department owns resolving a price variance between the PO and the supplier invoice?
  • Warehouse
  • Finance AR
  • Purchasing and Finance AP together (correct)
  • IT
Session 2 - End-to-End Process Walkthroughs

Order to Cash — Full Walkthrough

90 min

Practice this session using the live portal steps below.

Practice Tasks
1. Verify customer — CRS610

Sales / CS opens CRS610 and confirms the customer is active, reviews payment terms, price list, and credit status.

2. Create order header — OIS100

Sales / CS opens OIS100, clicks New for a new order. Enters customer number, order type, ship-to address, requested delivery date, and customer PO number.

3. Add order lines — OIS101

Sales / CS adds at least two lines: item, quantity, UOM, price, and delivery date. Verifies M3 auto-populated the correct price from the customer's price list.

4. Check availability — MMS200

Sales / CS or Warehouse checks available quantity in MMS200. If stock is short, a backorder decision is made and communicated to the customer before proceeding.

5. Confirm shipment — OIS300

Warehouse opens OIS300, finds the order, verifies items and ship-to address, enters actual shipped quantities, ship date, and carrier/tracking. Presses F5 to confirm — inventory updates and invoice is triggered.

6. Review customer invoice — ARS100

Finance AR opens ARS100, locates the invoice generated from the shipment. Verifies customer, order number, shipped quantity, and invoice amount.

7. Apply customer payment — ARS200

Finance AR opens ARS200, enters the customer payment, and applies it to the correct invoice. Verifies the invoice balance clears to zero.

8. Review aging if needed — ARS300

Finance AR opens ARS300 to confirm the customer's aging is updated. Flags any remaining overdue balances for follow-up.

9. Handle exception — backorder or credit hold

If backorder: Sales / CS documents and communicates delay. If credit hold: Sales / CS stops and contacts Finance AR before any customer communication.

10. Debrief — handoff questions

Group discusses: where do customer-facing problems start? Where do financial problems start? What does each department need from the previous step?

Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can trace all steps of the Order to Cash process from customer order to payment
  • I understand what each department must get right before the handoff
  • I know how to handle a backorder — availability check, decision, and customer communication
  • I know the credit hold process and that Sales / CS must contact Finance before communicating with the customer
  • I completed a proof-of-completion entry with customer, order, shipment, invoice, and payment references
Knowledge Check
1. Before Warehouse can ship an order, Sales / CS must ensure:
  • The payment is already collected
  • The customer invoice is generated
  • The customer order has correct items, quantities, ship-to address, and requested date (correct)
  • The AR aging is reviewed
2. Confirming a shipment in OIS300 automatically:
  • Creates a new customer order
  • Updates inventory and triggers the customer invoice (correct)
  • Sends an email to the customer
  • Closes the customer account
3. If a customer is on credit hold, Sales / CS should:
  • Ship the order and let Finance sort it out
  • Contact the customer directly to collect payment
  • Stop order processing and contact Finance AR before any customer communication (correct)
  • Cancel the order without notifying anyone
4. Applying a customer payment in ARS200 to the wrong invoice causes:
  • No problem — it can be corrected later without impact
  • The correct invoice to remain open and appear overdue, possibly triggering a credit hold (correct)
  • The payment to be lost
  • The order to be cancelled
