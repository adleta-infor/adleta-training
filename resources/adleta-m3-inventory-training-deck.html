Session 1 - Inventory and Stock Control

Item and Stock Lookup — MMS001, MMS200, MMS002, MWS068

45 min
Objective

Check the on-hand stock balance for an item in a specific warehouse in M3.

Scenario

A customer asks if you have stock of item ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE) available in the Carrollton warehouse. Look up the balance in MMS200.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MMS200 and filter by warehouse
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MMS200, and open Item. Open Toolbox.
  2. In the Filter Options, set Warehouse = CAR (Carrollton).
  3. In the Item number filter field, type ARM51830031.
  4. Click Apply to filter the list.
2. View the stock balance
  1. ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE) appears in the list with item type VCT and status 20.
  2. Right-click the row and select Related > Balance Identity to open MWS068.
  3. MWS068 shows the on-hand balance by location - note Location = CARE34V and On-hand approve = 24.
  4. This tells you there are 24 units on hand at location CARE34V in the CAR warehouse.
3. Interpret the balance
  1. On-hand approve = 24 means 24 units are approved and available.
  2. The Lot number field shows P236C - this is the lot the stock is assigned to.
  3. If the quantity is sufficient for the customer order, proceed to enter the order in OIS300.
  4. If stock is low, check Availability All Warehouses (Related > Availability All Warehouses) to see other locations.
Expected Outcomes
  • Stock balance checked in MMS200 for item ARM51830031.
  • Location CARE34V confirmed with 24 units on hand.
  • Availability confirmed before entering customer order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find an item in MMS200 and open MMS001
  • I can read MMS002 for one warehouse
  • I can find lot and location in MWS068
  • I promise available, not on hand
Knowledge Check
1. Which program shows exactly which rolls / lots are where?
  • MWS068 (correct)
  • MMS001
  • OIS100
  • PPS200
2. Item settings for one warehouse (on hand, issue multiple) are in:
  • MMS002 (correct)
  • CRS610
  • MWS420
  • GLS100
3. To promise stock to a customer, use:
  • On hand
  • Available (correct)
  • Last receipt
  • Reorder point
Session 2 - Inventory and Stock Control

Tracing Stock — Lots, Rolls, and History

45 min
Objective

Trace a stock movement for a roll that can't be found.

Scenario

MWS068 shows 24 of ARM51830031 at CARE34V, lot P236C, but the counter can only find 22. Trace what happened before anyone adjusts stock.

Practice Tasks
1. Confirm the book balance
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS068, and click OK.
  2. Filter item ARM51830031, warehouse CAR.
  3. Note location CARE34V, lot P236C, and the on-hand quantity.
2. Read the history
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS070, and click OK.
  2. Filter item ARM51830031 and warehouse CAR.
  3. Find the most recent movements for CARE34V: picks, receipts, moves. Note date and user.
3. Decide and log
  1. If a pick or move explains the difference, follow up with whoever did it.
  2. If nothing explains it, log the discrepancy (item, location, lot, M3 qty, physical qty) and hand it to inventory control for a count correction (inv-3).
Expected Outcomes
  • Book balance confirmed in MWS068.
  • Recent movements reviewed in MWS070.
  • Discrepancy explained or logged, not covered up.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open one roll or lot in MMS060
  • I can read stock history in MWS070
  • I investigate before correcting stock
Knowledge Check
1. Stock transaction history is in:
  • MWS070 (correct)
  • OIS350
  • PPS250
  • CRS620
2. Dye lot master and dates are in:
  • MMS235 (correct)
  • MMS310
  • OIS002
  • APS200
3. You can't find stock that M3 shows. First:
  • Adjust it out
  • Check MWS070 history and log it (correct)
  • Move it to another location
  • Ignore it
Session 3 - Inventory and Stock Control

Count Corrections — MMS310

60 min
Objective

Perform a physical inventory count and enter the count result in M3.

Scenario

A cycle count is being performed at CAR warehouse, location CARE34V. The physical count for item ARM51830031 shows 22 units. Enter the count in M3 and review the variance.

Practice Tasks
1. Check the current book balance
  1. Open MMS200, set Warehouse = CAR, filter Item number = ARM51830031, click Apply.
  2. Right-click ARM51830031 and select Related > Balance Identity (MWS068).
  3. Confirm current On-hand approve = 24 at location CARE34V, lot P236C.
  4. Note this as your book quantity before the count.
2. Enter the counted quantity
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MMS310, and click OK.
  2. Enter warehouse CAR, item ARM51830031, location CARE34V, lot P236C.
  3. Enter the counted quantity = 22.
  4. Check the difference shown (24 book vs 22 counted = -2) before confirming.
3. Review the variance and approve
  1. Confirm the entry only after the difference is approved.
  2. M3 adjusts on hand to 22 at CARE34V.
  3. Return to MWS068 to confirm the balance, and MWS070 to see the adjustment transaction.
Expected Outcomes
  • Count correction entered in MMS310 for ARM51830031 at CARE34V.
  • Difference of -2 reviewed and approved.
  • Balance confirmed in MWS068.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I check history and get approval before correcting
  • I can enter a count correction in MMS310
  • I verify the result in MWS068 and MWS070
  • I tell Finance about write-offs
Knowledge Check
1. Count corrections are entered in:
  • MMS310 (correct)
  • MMS100
  • PPS300
  • OIS101
2. Before correcting stock you should:
  • Check MWS070 and get approval (correct)
  • Just enter it
  • Move stock to cover it
  • Delete the lot
3. After the correction, check:
  • MWS068 and MWS070 (correct)
  • CRS620
  • GLS990
  • OIS350

Transfers Between Warehouses — MMS100 and MMS101

60 min
Objective

Move stock from Carrollton to Little Rock.

Scenario

Little Rock needs 10 CT of ARM51830031 from Carrollton (CAR). Create the transfer.

Practice Tasks
1. Check stock
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MMS002, and click OK.
  2. Check ARM51830031 at CAR has at least 10 available.
2. Create the distribution order
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MMS100, and click OK.
  2. Click + (New). From warehouse CAR, to warehouse Little Rock (ask your trainer for the code).
  3. Enter the date needed and save. Note the order number.
  4. In MMS101 add item ARM51830031, quantity 10. Save.
3. Follow it
  1. Open MWS410 at CAR and find the delivery for the distribution order.
  2. Note its status and tell your trainer it's ready for the warehouse.
Expected Outcomes
  • Distribution order created with the line.
  • Delivery visible in MWS410 at CAR.
Exception Handling
The to warehouse code isn't accepted
Ask your trainer for the exact Little Rock warehouse code in M3.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can create a distribution order in MMS100 / MMS101
  • I check available stock first
  • I know it's picked and received like other deliveries
Knowledge Check
1. Transfers between warehouses use:
  • MMS100 / MMS101 distribution orders (correct)
  • MMS310
  • OIS100
  • PPS200
2. Before adding a transfer line, check:
  • Available stock in the from warehouse (correct)
  • GL balance
  • Customer credit
  • Nothing
3. A distribution order is picked using:
  • MWS410 / MWS420 (correct)
  • APS100
  • CRS620
  • GLS100
Session 5 - Inventory and Stock Control

Supply, Demand, and Planning Settings — MMS080 and MMS002

45 min
Objective

Review and update planning settings for an item.

Scenario

The buyer wants a new reorder point and order quantity for ARM51830031 at CAR.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the item in the warehouse
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MMS002, and click OK.
  2. Filter warehouse CAR and item ARM51830031.
  3. Right-click the row and choose Change.
2. Review settings
  1. Find reorder point, order quantity, and safety stock (use the panel tabs at the bottom).
  2. Write down the current values.
3. Update and check
  1. Enter the new values from the buyer and save.
  2. Reopen the record to confirm they saved.
  3. Open MMS080 for the item and CAR to see current supply and demand.
Expected Outcomes
  • Planning settings reviewed and updated in MMS002.
  • Values confirmed saved.
  • Supply and demand reviewed in MMS080.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can read MMS080 to answer "when is it coming"
  • I know planning settings are in MMS002
  • I check the change saved and shows in planning
Knowledge Check
1. Supply and demand by date for an item:
  • MMS080 (correct)
  • MWS070
  • OIS350
  • CRS620
2. Reorder point and order quantity live in:
  • MMS002 (correct)
  • OIS100
  • PPS390
  • GLS200
3. Planning settings drive:
  • Planned POs in PPS170 (correct)
  • Customer invoices
  • AR aging
  • Cash desk
