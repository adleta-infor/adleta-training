Installation Supply Center / Will Call

All pickups happen at the ISC. Customer Service takes the order and sets it to will call (WC); the ISC stages it, takes payment at the counter, hands it over, handles walk-in retail sales, and closes the drawer at the end of the day.

Session 1 - Installation Supply Center / Will Call

Customer Pickup at the ISC — Paid and Prepaid Will Calls

1 hour
Objective

Hand over a will call order at the ISC and verify the order closes correctly in M3.

Scenario

A customer arrives at the ISC to pick up their will call order. Confirm the order is ready, verify status, and understand how the order closes after pickup.

Practice Tasks
1. Look up the will call order in OIS300
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS300, and press Enter. In the Customer box type the customer number and press Enter. To practice, use cash account 000750 and find CO 1000000438.
  2. Read Lst and Hi. 33-ALLOCATED means it hasn't been released for picking yet. 44-PICK RELEASED means it's been released and can be pulled.
  3. Still at 33? Click the order, click Delivery toolbox in the right-hand panel, click the delivery, click Display, and scroll to Dely stop code. C10-Delivery not paid means payment hasn't been taken. Blank means there's no payment hold; it's just waiting to be released. (1000000438 shows C10 on delivery 445. Look only: don't change it.)
  4. Confirm the goods are staged at the ISC and ready. Don't hand anything over until it's paid and released.
2. Confirm pickup details
  1. Open the order in OIS100 and verify all lines are correct.
  2. Confirm quantity, item, and any special handling notes.
  3. If payment is due at pickup, confirm payment terms and collect payment before releasing goods.
3. Verify order closes after pickup
  1. After the ISC hands it over, check OIS300 for the order.
  2. Check the status advances after the warehouse reports the pick and issues the delivery. Confirm with your trainer which final status to expect.
  3. Verify the transaction posts correctly in AR.
Expected Outcomes
  • Will call order read correctly: released (44), or held for payment (C10 on the delivery).
  • Payment taken at the ISC counter before release.
  • Order status followed through pickup.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I check the order is staged at the ISC and who is picking it up
  • I can find the Dely stop code on the delivery from OIS300
  • I know 33 means not released yet and 44 means released for picking
  • I know payment is taken in OIS215
Knowledge Check
1. A will call is still 33-ALLOCATED. Where do you look to see if it's waiting for payment?
  • The credit flag in OIS300
  • Dely stop code on the delivery (Delivery toolbox → Display) (correct)
  • CRS610
  • OIS101
2. Which program takes the counter payment?
  • OIS215 (correct)
  • OIS120
  • ARS200
  • APS100
3. After the payment is taken:
  • The order must be re-entered
  • The C10 stop comes off and the delivery can be released (correct)
  • AR posts a journal
  • Nothing changes
Session 2 - Installation Supply Center / Will Call

Taking a Payment at the Cash Desk — OIS215

1 hour
Objective

Key a cash sale for a walk-in customer and take the payment in OIS215.

Scenario

A walk-in customer with no account is at the Carrollton counter and wants 2 CT of ARM51830031. Key it on the Carrollton cash sale account and take the payment on cash desk 101A.

Practice Tasks
1. Key the cash sale
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS300, and press Enter. Click New order.
  2. Enter Customer = 000750 (CASH SALES/ CARROLLTON) and click Next.
  3. Click Next to Generic Panel. Check Payment terms = COD and Delivery method = WC. Leave them as they are.
  4. Click Next. In Customer's ord, type your initials-CD1, like LM-CD1 and click Next until you reach the order lines.
  5. Add ARM51830031, quantity 2, and click Add. The line shows 22-Reserved and Ptm COD.
  6. Note the CO no and the total for the customer.
2. Take the payment
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS215, and press Enter.
  2. Select cash desk 101A.
  3. Find the order or invoice for 000750 and confirm the amount with the customer.
  4. Select the payment method the customer is using and save.
3. Confirm the order can move
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS410, and press Enter.
  2. Find the delivery for the CO no.
  3. If the order was on a pay-first term, confirm the C10 stop is gone.
  4. Tell the warehouse it is paid and ready to release.
Expected Outcomes
  • Payment recorded on cash desk 101A.
  • Delivery free of the C10 stop.
  • Warehouse told the order is ready.
Exception Handling
The customer is at Little Rock
Key it on 000755 CASH SALES/ LITTLE ROCK. Little Rock has no cash desk in M3 yet, so ask your lead how to take the payment.
Cash desk 101A does not appear
Your user may not be connected to that desk (OIS212). Tell your trainer.
The payment method you need is not offered
The desk only accepts the methods connected in OIS211. Tell your trainer. Do not use a different method to get around it.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can key a cash sale on the cash account for my location
  • I can open OIS215 on the right cash desk
  • I confirm the order/invoice and amount before taking money
  • I pick the right payment method
  • I know taking payment on a prepay order releases its C10 stop
Knowledge Check
1. Which program takes a payment at the counter?
  • OIS215 (correct)
  • OIS217
  • OIS120
  • ARS110
2. Where do cash desks exist today?
  • All three warehouses
  • Carrollton only (101A, 101B) (correct)
  • Lenexa only
  • None
3. Can M3 stop a cash-only customer paying by check?
  • Yes, automatically
  • No. There is no per-customer switch; the clerk must catch it (correct)
  • Only on Fridays
  • Only for COD
4. A walk-in customer at Carrollton has no account. Which customer do you key the order on?
  • 000382
  • 000750 CASH SALES/ CARROLLTON (correct)
  • 000765 PROSHOP
  • Create a new customer
Session 3 - Installation Supply Center / Will Call

Prepay Orders — Why the Order Isn't Moving

45 min
Objective

Recognize and clear a prepay hold.

Scenario

A test customer is set to Payment terms PCD in company 100. Key a small C01 order and follow it until it can be picked.

Practice Tasks
1. Key the order
  1. Enter a small C01 order for a stocked item for the PCD test customer (your trainer provides it).
  2. Finish the order and note the CO no.
2. Find the hold
  1. In OIS300, confirm the order shows no credit flag.
  2. From OIS300, click the order, click Delivery toolbox, click the delivery, click Display, and look at Dely stop code for C10-Delivery not paid.
3. Clear it
  1. Take the payment in OIS215 on cash desk 101A.
  2. Re-check MWS410: the C10 stop should be gone and the delivery can be released.
  3. Write down what you saw. This test also settles open points in Mike's prepay guide.
Expected Outcomes
  • C10 stop seen on a PCD order.
  • No credit flag in OIS300.
  • Stop cleared after payment in OIS215.
Exception Handling
No C10 stop appears
Note it and tell Mike. The order type or a prepayment selection table may also matter. This is one of the things the test is checking.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can explain the prepay flow end to end
  • I check MWS410 for C10 when a prepay order isn't moving
  • I know PCD and CSH stop for payment, and I ask about COD
Knowledge Check
1. A prepay order allocates but won't release. The stop on the delivery is:
  • C10 (correct)
  • 33
  • CL1
  • C05
2. Does a prepay hold show as a credit flag in OIS300?
  • Yes
  • No (correct)
  • Only for COD
  • Only after 30 days
3. Where do you see C10 on an order?
  • The OIS300 credit flag
  • Dely stop code on the order's delivery (correct)
  • CRS610 panel J
  • OIS101
Session 4 - Installation Supply Center / Will Call

End of Day — Closing the Drawer

45 min
Objective

Close the cash drawer at the end of the day.

Scenario

It's end of day at the Carrollton counter. Print the day's payments for desk 101A, reconcile, and validate the desk.

Practice Tasks
1. Print the payments
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS223, and press Enter.
  2. Select desk 101A and today's date and run it.
2. Reconcile
  1. Count cash, checks, and card slips.
  2. Compare each total to the OIS223 list.
  3. Write down any difference before going further.
3. Validate
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS217, and press Enter.
  2. Validate desk 101A for today with your trainer watching.
  3. Run OIS221 to list any unpaid cash invoices.
Expected Outcomes
  • Day's payments printed.
  • Drawer reconciled by payment method.
  • Desk validated in OIS217.
Exception Handling
The drawer doesn't match OIS223
Recount first. If it still doesn't match, stop and tell your manager before validating.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can print the day's payments in OIS223
  • I count and reconcile the drawer by payment method
  • I can validate the desk in OIS217 with my trainer
  • I can list unpaid cash invoices in OIS221
