Session 1 - Will Call Counter and Cash Desk

Taking a Payment at the Cash Desk — OIS215

1 hour
Objective

Take a counter payment for a will call order in OIS215.

Scenario

Customer 000382 is at the Carrollton counter to pay for and pick up a will call order of 3 CT of ARM51830031. Take the payment on cash desk 101A.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the order
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS300, and click OK.
  2. Filter Customer = 000382 and find the will call order (delivery method WC).
  3. Note the CO no and order total.
2. Take the payment
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS215, and click OK.
  2. Select cash desk 101A.
  3. Find the order or invoice for 000382 and confirm the amount with the customer.
  4. Select the payment method the customer is using and save.
3. Confirm the order can move
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type MWS410, and click OK.
  2. Find the delivery for the CO no.
  3. If the order was on a pay-first term, confirm the C10 stop is gone.
  4. Tell the warehouse it is paid and ready to release.
Expected Outcomes
  • Payment recorded on cash desk 101A.
  • Delivery free of the C10 stop.
  • Warehouse told the order is ready.
Exception Handling
Cash desk 101A does not appear
Your user may not be connected to that desk (OIS212). Tell your trainer.
The payment method you need is not offered
The desk only accepts the methods connected in OIS211. Tell your trainer. Do not use a different method to get around it.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open OIS215 on the right cash desk
  • I confirm the order/invoice and amount before taking money
  • I pick the right payment method
  • I know taking payment on a prepay order releases its C10 stop

  • All three warehouses

Session 2 - Will Call Counter and Cash Desk

Prepay Orders — Why the Order Isn't Moving

45 min
Objective

Recognize and clear a prepay hold.

Scenario

A test customer is set to Payment terms PCD in company 100. Key a small C01 order and follow it until it can be picked.

Practice Tasks
1. Key the order
  1. Enter a small C01 order for a stocked item for the PCD test customer (your trainer provides it).
  2. Finish the order and note the CO no.
2. Find the hold
  1. In OIS300, confirm the order shows no credit flag.
  2. In MWS410, find the delivery. Look for stop C10 "delivery not paid".
3. Clear it
  1. Take the payment in OIS215 on cash desk 101A.
  2. Re-check MWS410: the C10 stop should be gone and the delivery can be released.
  3. Write down what you saw. This test also settles open points in Mike's prepay guide.
Expected Outcomes
  • C10 stop seen on a PCD order.
  • No credit flag in OIS300.
  • Stop cleared after payment in OIS215.
Exception Handling
No C10 stop appears
Note it and tell Mike. The order type or a prepayment selection table may also matter. This is one of the things the test is checking.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can explain the prepay flow end to end
  • I check MWS410 for C10 when a prepay order isn't moving
  • I know which terms stop for payment and which are labels only
Knowledge Check
1. A prepay order allocates but won't release. The stop on the delivery is:
  • C10 (correct)
  • 33
  • CL1
  • C05
2. Does a prepay hold show as a credit flag in OIS300?
  • Yes
  • No (correct)
  • Only for COD
  • Only after 30 days
3. Which terms actually make the order wait for payment today?
  • COD and PP

Session 3 - Will Call Counter and Cash Desk

End of Day — Closing the Drawer

45 min
Objective

Close the cash drawer at the end of the day.

Scenario

It's end of day at the Carrollton counter. Print the day's payments for desk 101A, reconcile, and validate the desk.

Practice Tasks
1. Print the payments
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS223, and click OK.
  2. Select desk 101A and today's date and run it.
2. Reconcile
  1. Count cash, checks, and card slips.
  2. Compare each total to the OIS223 list.
  3. Write down any difference before going further.
3. Validate
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS217, and click OK.
  2. Validate desk 101A for today with your trainer watching.
  3. Run OIS221 to list any unpaid cash invoices.
Expected Outcomes
  • Day's payments printed.
  • Drawer reconciled by payment method.
  • Desk validated in OIS217.
Exception Handling
The drawer doesn't match OIS223
Recount first. If it still doesn't match, stop and tell your manager before validating.
Sign-Off Checklist

  • I count and reconcile the drawer by payment method
  • I can validate the desk in OIS217 with my trainer
  • I can list unpaid cash invoices in OIS221
