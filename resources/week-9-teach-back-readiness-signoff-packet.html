Session 1 - Trainer Readiness and Go-Live Prep

Teach-Back Preparation

60 min

Practice this session using the live portal steps below.

Practice Tasks
1. Review your module

Open your module in the portal and review every session, step, quiz, and checklist. Identify any gaps before your teach-back.

2. Know your 15-minute structure

2 min: explain the department workflow. 5 min: walk through key M3 programs. 3 min: explain one exception and first response. 2 min: explain a department handoff. 2 min: give a practice question you would ask users. 1 min: confirm escalation path.

3. Prepare your normal scenario

Choose one complete normal workflow you can walk through live in M3 — from opening the program to saving the transaction.

4. Prepare your exception scenario

Choose one common exception in your module. Know the steps: identify it, who to contact, how to log it.

5. Prepare your handoff

Name one department that hands off to you and one you hand off to. Know what data must be correct at each handoff.

6. Prepare a department training tip

Think about one thing your department users will find confusing. Plan how you would explain or demonstrate it.

7. Practice with a peer

Run through your 15-minute teach-back with another super user. Time yourself. Get feedback on clarity.

8. Review the rubric

Passing requires average 2.5 or higher across all criteria and no score of 1 in navigation, transaction accuracy, or trainer readiness.

Sign-Off Checklist
  • I have reviewed every session, step, and checklist in my assigned module
  • I know my 15-minute teach-back structure
  • I have a normal scenario I can walk through live in M3
  • I have an exception scenario with the correct handling steps
  • I know my upstream and downstream department handoffs
  • I have practiced with a peer and received feedback
  • I understand the rubric and passing standard
Knowledge Check
1. The teach-back format is 15 minutes. The first 2 minutes should cover:
  • Walking through every screen in your module
  • Explaining the department workflow in plain business language (correct)
  • Reading from your training notes
  • Showing the issue log template
2. The passing standard for the teach-back rubric is:
  • Perfect scores on all criteria
  • Average of 2.5 or higher and no score of 1 in navigation, accuracy, or trainer readiness (correct)
  • Completing all sessions in the portal
  • Attending all 9 weeks of training
3. If you are scored 1 (Not Ready) on System Navigation, you:
  • Still pass if your other scores are high enough
  • Do not pass regardless of other scores (correct)
  • Can retake only that section
  • Are automatically assigned to IT support
4. The best way to prepare for your teach-back is:
  • Memorize the program codes
  • Practice the full 15-minute walkthrough with a peer and time yourself
  • Read the training documents the night before (correct)
  • Ask Kristen to show you the workflow again
Session 2 - Trainer Readiness and Go-Live Prep

Teach-Back and Readiness Sign-Off

90 min

Practice this session using the live portal steps below.

Practice Tasks
1. Deliver your 15-minute teach-back

Present to KRISTENB and the group. Follow the structure: workflow, programs, exception, handoff, practice question, escalation path.

2. Receive rubric scores

KRISTENB scores each criteria 1-3. Listen for feedback. Do not argue — note coaching items for remediation.

3. Review readiness decision

Ready: proceed to department training plan. Ready With Coaching: targeted remediation assigned before go-live. Not Ready: additional training scheduled with backup support assigned.

4. Build your FAQ contribution

Write 3-5 questions your department users are likely to ask. Include the correct answer and which program or step it relates to. Submit to the shared FAQ.

5. Review open issues

Go through the issue log. For each open item in your module: confirm owner, confirm resolution path, and flag anything that could block go-live.

6. Sign readiness acknowledgment

Complete the readiness acknowledgment form: name, department, module, readiness status, required remediation, and signature. Manager countersigns.

7. Confirm department training date

Lock in the date, location, and attendee list for your department training session. Confirm practice data is ready.

Sign-Off Checklist
  • I delivered my 15-minute teach-back
  • I received my rubric scores and understand any coaching feedback
  • I know my readiness status — Ready, Ready With Coaching, or Not Ready
  • I submitted 3-5 FAQ contributions with correct answers
  • I reviewed all open issues in my module and confirmed owners
  • I signed the readiness acknowledgment
  • My department training date and attendee list are confirmed
Knowledge Check
1. If you receive a readiness status of Ready With Coaching, your next step is:
  • Wait for go-live and figure it out on the job
  • Complete assigned remediation and retest the weak area before go-live (correct)
  • Start training your department immediately
  • Request a different module assignment
2. Your FAQ contribution should include:
  • Every possible M3 question
  • 3-5 likely questions from your department users with correct answers and program references (correct)
  • Only questions you personally had during training
  • A copy of all training documents
3. The readiness acknowledgment confirms that you:
  • Scored perfectly on all quizzes
  • Attended every training session without absence
  • Completed training, practiced, finished your teach-back, and understand your go-live responsibility (correct)
  • Have read all SharePoint documents
4. Before your department training session, you must confirm:
  • That all super users have passed their teach-backs
  • Training date, attendees, practice data, and that your known issues are documented (correct)
  • That the portal has been updated
  • That IT has finished the system configuration
Session 3 - Trainer Readiness and Go-Live Prep

Go-Live Support and Department Handoff

60 min

Practice this session using the live portal steps below.

Practice Tasks
1. Know your support tier

You are Tier 1 — first-line support for your department. Handle how-to questions, navigation, and known workarounds. Escalate to KRISTENB (Tier 2) when an issue blocks work, affects data, or needs a configuration change.

2. Know the issue log fields

Every issue must include: date/time, department, reported by, program, process, what happened, steps tried, business impact, priority (High/Medium/Low), owner, status, and resolution.

3. Understand issue priority

High: blocks orders, shipments, receiving, invoicing, payment, or period close — same day triage. Medium: workaround exists but slows work — 1-2 days. Low: question, training gap, or enhancement — weekly review.

4. Know the daily go-live rhythm

Start of day: review open high-priority issues. Midday: check blockers. End of day: review resolved items and FAQ updates. Report to KRISTENB if anything is blocking your department.

5. Confirm your floor support availability

Know your agreed support hours during go-live week. Keep your module guide and quick reference open. Use training materials before guessing.

6. Review your department training handoff checklist

Confirm: department user list, training date, quick reference ready, practice scenario ready, known issues documented, escalation path shared, sign-off method confirmed.

7. Avoid unauthorized changes

Never make data or configuration changes in production without approval. Log the issue and escalate. Document everything.

8. Post go-live stabilization

For the first 2-4 weeks: continue daily issue review, track repeated training gaps, update job aids weekly, hold department check-ins, review open items with leadership weekly.

Sign-Off Checklist
  • I know my support tier and what I handle vs what I escalate
  • I can create a complete issue-log entry with all required fields
  • I know the three priority levels and response expectations
  • I know the daily go-live rhythm and when to report to KRISTENB
  • I have confirmed my floor support availability during go-live week
  • My department training handoff checklist is complete
  • I understand I must never make unauthorized data or configuration changes in production
Knowledge Check
1. As a Tier 1 super user, you should escalate an issue to KRISTENB when:
  • A department user asks a how-to question
  • The issue blocks work, affects data, or requires a configuration or security change (correct)
  • Someone is confused about navigation
  • You want to update the FAQ
2. A High priority issue is one that:
  • Is annoying but has a workaround
  • Blocks orders, shipments, receiving, invoicing, payment, or period close (correct)
  • Is a training gap or question
  • Was logged more than once
3. If a department user asks you how to do something and you are not sure, you should:
  • Give your best guess
  • Tell them to call IT
  • Check your training materials first before answering (correct)
  • Ignore the question until you are certain
4. During the post go-live stabilization period, repeated questions should become:
  • Ignored after the first week
  • FAQ updates and job aid improvements (correct)
  • Escalations to Infor support
  • New training sessions from scratch
