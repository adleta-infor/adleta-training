Session 1 - Customer Service and Order Entry

Manual Order Entry — Standard Net Terms (CL1)

60 min
Objective

Enter a standard customer sales order in M3 using order type C01.

Scenario

Customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) calls in an order for 5 CT of item ARM51830031 (IMPERIAL TEXTURE 1/8 INCH TILE). Enter the order.

Practice Tasks
1. Open OIS100 and find the customer
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start. Type OIS100, select Customer Order. Open from the list, and click OK.
  2. The Account Search box pops up. Type 000382 and press Tab (you can also search by name, city, or phone). Click the customer.
  3. Once the customer is found, the header fills in automatically: Customer, Payer, CO type C01, Req delivery dt, and Facility 101. Review it.
  4. Need a different delivery date? Click the calendar icon. The Delivery calendar shows the customer's route, delivery days, and the 3:00 PM cutoff the day before. Pick a delivery day and click Close.
  5. Click Next.
2. Fill in Order Details (OIS100/I)
  1. Check the defaults: Warehouse CAR, Priority 5-Norm, Payment terms N30, Delivery method OT (Our Truck).
  2. In Customer's ord, enter the customer's PO number. This field is required.
  3. Click Next.
3. Enter the order line (OIS101)
  1. Use the entry row under the grid. Whs defaults to CAR, Ln tp stays 0, and Req dt carries over from the header.
  2. Enter Item ARM51830031. Don't know the item number? Open Item search, type part of the number or name, and click Search. Check the CAR column for stock (green means available), then click Pick stock.
  3. Enter Order qty 5 and confirm U/M = CT. Sales price fills in from the customer price list.
  4. Click Add. The line saves right away. There is no separate Save button. The line drops into the grid and the Running Total updates.
  5. Note the CO no (order number) for follow-up.
4. Verify the order in OIS300
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start. Type OIS300, select it from the list, and click OK.
  2. In the Customer filter, type 000382 and press Enter.
  3. Find your CO no. Lst (lowest status) and Hi (highest status) should both read 33-ALLOCATED. That means the stock is reserved and the order is ready for the warehouse.
  4. If your order shows 20-Line Not Entered, the header was saved with no lines. Click the CO no to open it and add the lines, or tell your trainer so it can be deleted.
  5. Next stop is the warehouse. A 33-ALLOCATED order sits in MWS410 (Delivery. Open Toolbox) showing 0-Release until its pick list is released. Once released, OIS300 shows 44-PICK RELEASED.
Expected Outcomes
  • Sales order created in M3 with CO type C01.
  • Customer PO entered in Customer's ord.
  • Order line entered with correct item, quantity, and price.
  • Order shows 33-ALLOCATED in OIS300 and is waiting in MWS410 for pick release.
  • CO number noted for follow-up.
Exception Handling
Alert: Customer's order number must be entered
Click OK, enter the customer's PO in Customer's ord on the Order Details screen, then click Next.
OIS300 shows 20-Line Not Entered
The order header exists but no lines were added. Open the CO and add the lines, or have your trainer delete the empty order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I open programs with Ctrl+R (Search and Start)
  • I can start an order in OIS100 from Account Search and check the header defaults
  • I always fill in Customer's ord (the customer PO)
  • I can add lines in OIS101 and know they save when I click Add
  • I can find my order in OIS300 and read 33-ALLOCATED vs 20-Line Not Entered
Knowledge Check
1. When you enter a customer number in OIS100, M3 automatically fills in:
  • Item prices only
  • Name, address, payment terms, and price list (correct)
  • Warehouse location and carrier
  • Nothing — you fill all fields manually
2. The customer PO number field is important because:
  • It is required to release the order
  • It prints on the invoice and packing slip for customer reference (correct)
  • It auto-populates the price list
  • It routes the order to the correct warehouse
3. A new order shows 33-ALLOCATED in OIS300. That means:
  • The order has shipped
  • Stock is reserved and the order is waiting for pick release in MWS410 (correct)
  • The order is on credit hold
  • The header saved without lines

Customer Return — Credit Order (C05)

Objective

Create a return/credit order in M3 for goods being sent back by a customer.

Scenario

Customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) is returning 2 CT of item ARM51830031 due to damage. Create a credit order using CO type C05.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the original order in OIS300
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and search for customer 000382.
  2. Locate the original order containing the item being returned.
  3. Note the CO no of the original order.
2. Create the return order
  1. Click + (New) in OIS300. OIS100/A opens.
  2. Enter Customer = 000382.
  3. Enter CO type = C05 (Credit NoStkUpd).

  5. Click Next.
3. Enter the return line and confirm
  1. In OIS101, enter Item = ARM51830031.
  2. Enter Order qty = 2 (the quantity being returned).
  3. Confirm U/M = CT.
  4. In Your ref 1 on OIS100/I, enter the original CO no for reference.
  5. Click Add and note the new credit order CO no.
Expected Outcomes

  • Original order referenced on the credit order.

2. CO type C05 is:
  • A quote

3. Where do you record the original order number on the credit order?

Session 3 - Customer Service and Order Entry

Order Entry — When an Order Is Held for Credit

60 min
Objective

Understand what happens when a customer order is stopped due to a credit limit in M3.

Scenario

You enter an order for a customer who has exceeded their credit limit. M3 places a CO stop on the order. Learn what the stop looks like and who to contact.

Practice Tasks
1. Enter the order
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and click + (New). OIS100/A opens.
  2. Enter Customer = 000382, CO type = C01.
  3. Enter Req delivery dt and click Next.
  4. Add an order line in OIS101 and click Add.
2. Identify the credit stop
  1. Return to OIS100/I (Generic Panel) for the order.
  2. Look at the CO stop field - a value of 3 means Credit limit stop.
  3. A CO stop prevents the order from advancing to picking and shipping.
  4. Do NOT release the stop without authorization from AR.
3. Escalate to AR
  1. Contact the AR team with the customer number, CO no, and order amount.
  2. AR will review the account and either release the stop or ask the customer to pay.
  3. Once released, the CO stop field will clear and the order can proceed.
  4. Add a note in Your ref 1 with the date and who approved the release.
Expected Outcomes
  • Credit stop identified on the order (CO stop = 3).
  • AR team contacted with order details.
  • Process for release understood.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can recognize a credit stop on an order
  • I can read (not change) CRS610 panel J and K
  • I escalate to credit and never work around a hold
  • I know a prepay hold shows as C10 on the delivery, not as a credit flag
  • I document who I called and when
Knowledge Check
1. Who releases a credit-held order?
  • The CSR who entered it
  • Credit / AR, in OIS120 (correct)
  • The warehouse
  • IT
2. Where are a customer's credit limits and payment terms?
  • OIS101
  • CRS610 panel J (correct)
  • MMS001
  • PPS200
3. A prepay order isn't moving and OIS300 shows no credit flag. Where do you look?
  • CRS620
  • MWS410, for a C10 stop on the delivery (correct)
  • GLS200
  • OIS010

Objective

Enter a will call order for a customer picking up at the Carrollton counter.

Scenario

Customer 000382 walks in and wants to pick up 3 CT of ARM51830031 today. Enter a will call order in OIS300 using CO type C01.

  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and click + (New). OIS100/A opens.
  2. Enter Customer = 000382.
  3. Enter CO type = C01 (Bravo Normal/WC - WC stands for Will Call).

  5. Click Next.

  1. In OIS101, enter Item = ARM51830031, Order qty = 3, confirm U/M = CT.

Payment Terms on an Order — COD, Prepay, and Credit Card

1 hour
Objective

Enter a customer order where payment terms are different from the account default.

Scenario

A customer wants to pay COD or with a credit card instead of their standard net terms. Enter the order and change the payment terms in OIS100/I.

Practice Tasks
1. Create the order header
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and click + (New). OIS100/A opens.
  2. Enter Customer number and CO type = C01.
  3. Enter Req delivery dt and click Next.
2. Change payment terms (OIS100/I)
  1. On OIS100/I, find Payment terms (defaults from the customer, usually N30).
  2. Click the search icon next to Payment terms to see the available terms.
  3. For a pay-first order pick PCD (Prep Cash Desk). Ask first: changing terms at order entry isn't settled.
  4. Note Payment mtd AR. It is a default only (most customers show CH1); it doesn't block anything.
  5. Click Next.
3. Enter order line and confirm
  1. In OIS101, enter the item, quantity, and confirm U/M.
  2. Click Add.
  3. Note the CO no and communicate the updated payment terms to the customer.
  4. Open MWS410 and find the delivery for your order. A PCD order should show stop C10 until the cash desk takes payment.
Expected Outcomes
  • Order entered with non-standard payment terms.
  • C10 stop seen on the delivery in MWS410 for a pay-first term.
  • CO number recorded.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I know prepay comes from payment terms (PCD, CSH), not a checkbox
  • I know COD is a label only at Adleta today
  • I can spot a C10 stop on a prepay delivery in MWS410
  • I ask before changing payment terms on an order
Knowledge Check
1. What makes a customer prepay in M3?
  • A prepay checkbox on the customer
  • Their payment terms (correct)
  • The warehouse
  • The CO type alone
2. At Adleta today, an order on COD terms:
  • Stops until paid
  • Goes to the warehouse like any other order (correct)
  • Cannot be saved
  • Goes on credit hold
3. A prepay delivery is held with which stop in MWS410?
  • C01
  • C10 "delivery not paid" (correct)
  • 33
  • CL1

Will Call Order — Pay at the Counter

1 hour
Objective

Process a will call order pickup and verify the order closes correctly in M3.

Scenario

A customer arrives to pick up their will call order. Confirm the order is ready, verify status, and understand how the order closes after pickup.

Practice Tasks
1. Look up the will call order in OIS300
  1. Press Ctrl+R to open Search and Start, type OIS300, select it from the list, click OK, and search by customer number or CO no.
  2. Check the status. A paid will call order that has been released shows 44-PICK RELEASED. If it is still 33-ALLOCATED, check MWS410 for a C10 stop (payment not taken yet).
  3. Confirm warehouse has the goods staged and ready.
2. Confirm pickup details
  1. Open the order in OIS100 and verify all lines are correct.
  2. Confirm quantity, item, and any special handling notes.
  3. If payment is due at pickup, confirm payment terms and collect payment before releasing goods.
3. Verify order closes after pickup
  1. After warehouse processes the will call pickup, check OIS300 for the order.
  2. Check the status advances after the warehouse reports the pick and issues the delivery. Confirm with your trainer which final status to expect.
  3. Verify the transaction posts correctly in AR.
Expected Outcomes
  • Will call order confirmed released (44) or held for payment (C10).
  • Payment taken at the cash desk before release.
  • Order status followed through pickup.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can enter a will call order with a pay-first term
  • I expect the C10 stop until payment
  • I know payment is taken in OIS215
  • I can follow the order through release and pickup in OIS300
Knowledge Check
1. On a PCD will call order, why won't the delivery release?
  • It is on credit hold
  • C10: the delivery hasn't been paid (correct)
  • No stock
  • Wrong warehouse
2. Which program takes the counter payment?
  • OIS215 (correct)
  • OIS120
  • ARS200
  • APS100
3. After the payment is taken:
  • The order must be re-entered
  • The C10 stop comes off and the delivery can be released (correct)
  • AR posts a journal
  • Nothing changes

Session 9 - Customer Service and Order Entry

Answering Customer Questions — Order, Stock, and Invoice Lookups

1 hour
Objective

Answer common customer calls using M3 lookups only.

Scenario

Customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) calls with three questions: where is their latest order, do you have ARM51830031 in stock at Carrollton and in which lot, and can you resend last week's invoice.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the order
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS300, and click OK.
  2. Filter Customer = 000382 and press Enter.
  3. Read Lst and Hi on the latest CO no. 33 = allocated and waiting for pick release; 44 = pick released.
  4. If it is still 33 and the customer expected it, check the delivery in MWS410 for a stop.
2. Check stock and lot
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type MWS068, and click OK.
  2. Filter item ARM51830031 and warehouse CAR.
  3. Note each location, lot, and on-hand quantity (the training data shows CAR1A10, lot A1234).
  4. If nothing is on hand, open MMS080 for the item and CAR to see when supply is due.
3. Find the invoice
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS350, and click OK.
  2. Filter on customer 000382.
  3. Find the invoice for last week and note the invoice number.
Expected Outcomes
  • Order status read correctly from OIS300.
  • Stock, location, and lot confirmed in MWS068.
  • Invoice located in OIS350.
Exception Handling
OIS350 shows no invoice for the order
The order may not have been invoiced yet. Check its status in OIS300.
MWS068 shows stock but OIS101 Item search shows none available
The stock may be allocated to other orders. Ask your trainer to walk through allocation for that item.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can answer "where is my order" from OIS300
  • I can search lines across orders in OIS301
  • I can check when stock is coming in MMS080
  • I can check lots and locations in MWS068
  • I can find an invoice in OIS350
Knowledge Check
1. Which program shows order lines across many orders?
  • OIS300
  • OIS301 (correct)
  • OIS110
  • CRS610
2. A customer asks when stock is coming in. Use:
  • MMS080 Material Plan (correct)
  • OIS350
  • CRS620
  • GLS200
3. To see which lot / roll is in which location:
  • MWS068 (correct)
  • OIS002
  • APS100
  • OIS110
