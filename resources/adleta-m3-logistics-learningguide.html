Session 1 - Warehouse, Shipping, and Routes

The Delivery Toolbox — MWS410

60 min
Objective

Locate a delivery in MWS410 and review the pick list status for a customer order.

Scenario

A customer order for consignee 007822 is ready for picking in the CAR warehouse. Open MWS410 to find the delivery and review its status.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MWS410 and filter for the delivery
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS410, and open Delivery. Open Toolbox.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR (Carrollton) and click Apply.
  3. The list shows deliveries with columns: Dely no, Consignee, Dmt (Delivery method), Order no, and status.
  4. Locate the delivery for consignee 007822 - note the Delivery number and status (e.g., 1-Picking List Generated).
2. Review the delivery detail
  1. Click the Delivery number link to open the delivery detail.
  2. Review the delivery lines - confirm item, quantity, and warehouse location.
  3. Note the Delivery method (Dmt) field - OT means Our Truck, WC means Will Call.
  4. If status is 1-Picking List Generated, the pick list has been printed and picking is in progress.
3. Confirm pick status in MWS420
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS420, and open Picking List. Report.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR, Pick sts = 40-Picking, and click Apply.
  3. Locate the delivery in the list - confirm it shows the correct print date (Prt dt) and pick time.
  4. Once warehouse confirms picking is complete, the delivery status will advance to the next step.
Expected Outcomes
  • Delivery located in MWS410 for warehouse CAR.
  • Pick list status confirmed in MWS420.
  • Delivery detail reviewed with correct items and quantities.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find deliveries in MWS410 by warehouse
  • I can read delivery method and status
  • I know release for picking is a manual warehouse step
  • I recognize a C10 stop
Knowledge Check
1. Where do you release a delivery for picking?
  • MWS410 (correct)
  • OIS100
  • PPS300
  • CRS610
2. Stop C10 on a delivery means:
  • Short stock
  • Prepay order not yet paid (correct)
  • Credit hold
  • Wrong address
3. WC on a delivery means:
  • Will Call (correct)
  • Warehouse Count
  • Wire Check
  • Weight Class
Session 2 - Warehouse, Shipping, and Routes

Picking — Reporting Picked Quantities in MWS420 / MWS422

45 min
Objective

Print and review a pick list report in MWS420 for the CAR warehouse.

Scenario

The warehouse team needs the pick list for today's deliveries. Run MWS420 to print or display the picking list for CAR warehouse.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MWS420 and set filters
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS420, and open Picking List. Report.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR.
  3. Set Pick sts = 40-Picking to show orders actively being picked.
  4. Set Rep date = today's date.
  5. Click Apply - the list shows all deliveries with their delivery number, size (Sz), print date (Prt dt), and print time (Prt t).
2. Review the pick list
  1. The list shows columns: Dely no (delivery number), Sfx, Sz (size/carton count), Prt dt (print date), Prt t (print time), Pck t (pick time), Picker.
  2. Confirm the deliveries that need to go out today are listed.
  3. If a delivery is missing from the list, return to MWS410 and verify the delivery status.
  4. Click a delivery number to drill into the individual pick lines for that delivery.
3. Assign pickers and track progress
  1. Use the Picker field to assign a warehouse employee to a delivery if required.
  2. Once items are picked, the warehouse team enters the pick time (Pck t) to confirm completion.
  3. Deliveries with a pick time confirmed are ready for loading and shipment.
  4. Return to MWS410 to process the delivery confirmation once all lines are picked.
Expected Outcomes
  • Pick list report run in MWS420 for CAR warehouse.
  • Deliveries at pick status 40 reviewed.
  • Pick assignments tracked and confirmed.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open and filter pick lists in MWS420
  • I report what was actually picked
  • I pick the lot on the list
Knowledge Check
1. Picked quantities are reported in:
  • MWS420 (correct)
  • OIS101
  • PPS201
  • GLS100
2. Pick list line detail is in:
  • MWS422 (correct)
  • MMS001
  • OIS002
  • ARS200
3. You can only find 8 of 10 on the pick list. You report:
  • 10
  • 8 (correct)
  • 0
  • Nothing

Session 4 - Warehouse, Shipping, and Routes

Will Call — Release, Pick, and Hand Over

1 hour
Objective

Get a will call order from the system to the counter.

Scenario

A WC order for customer 000382 is in MWS410 at CAR. The customer is coming at 2 PM.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the delivery
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type MWS410, and click OK.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR and Apply.
  3. Find the delivery with Dmt = WC for 000382. Check for a C10 stop.
2. Release and pick
  1. If there is no stop, release it for picking.
  2. Open MWS420, find the pick list, pick the items, and report the picked quantities.
  3. Stage the goods at the counter.
3. Hand over
  1. When the customer arrives, check the order number.
  2. Hand over the goods and complete the issue step with your trainer.
  3. Check OIS300: the order status has moved on.
Expected Outcomes
  • Will call delivery released and picked.
  • Goods staged and handed over.
  • Order status updated in OIS300.
Exception Handling
The delivery has a C10 stop
The customer hasn't paid. Send them to the cash desk (OIS215) first.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I find will call deliveries in MWS410
  • I wait for payment on C10 deliveries
  • I release, pick, report, and stage will call orders
Knowledge Check
1. A will call delivery shows C10. You:
  • Hand over anyway
  • Wait until the cash desk takes payment (correct)
  • Cancel it
  • Change the terms
2. Will call deliveries are found in:
  • MWS410 (correct)
  • PPS220
  • GLS200
  • CRS620
3. After picking, report the pick in:
  • MWS420 (correct)
  • OIS215
  • APS100
  • MMS080

Delivery Questions and Common Carrier

45 min
Objective

Answer a customer's "where is my delivery" question.

Scenario

Consignee 007822 calls asking when their order will arrive.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the order
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS300, and click OK.
  2. Filter on customer 007822 and read the order's status.
2. Find the delivery
  1. Open MWS410, Warehouse = CAR, Apply.
  2. Find the delivery for 007822. Note status, Dmt (OT/WC/CC), and any stop.
3. Answer
  1. If OT, check which load (DRS100) and route it is on.
  2. If there is a stop, tell the customer why (for C10: payment needed).
  3. Note what you told the customer.
Expected Outcomes

Session 6 - Warehouse, Shipping, and Routes

Routes and Delivery Setup (Setup Users Only)

1 hour
Objective

Explain why a customer's delivery calendar shows certain days.

Scenario

CS says customer 000382 can only get deliveries on certain days. Find out why.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the customer's route
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type DRS011, and click OK.
  2. Find the selection line that gives 000382 its route. Note the route.
2. Find the departures
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type DRS006, and click OK.
  2. Filter on that route and note the departure days and times.
3. Compare
  1. In OIS100, start an order for 000382 and open the delivery calendar.
  2. Confirm the days match the departures. Close without saving.
Expected Outcomes
  • Customer's route found in DRS011.
  • Departure days found in DRS006.
  • Delivery calendar explained.
Exception Handling
You can't see DRS011 or DRS006
These are setup programs. Your role may not have access. Ask Mike.
