Session 1 - Logistics / Transportation

Pick and Pack — Will Call Order

60 min
Objective

Locate a delivery in MWS410 and review the pick list status for a customer order.

Scenario

A customer order for consignee 007822 is ready for picking in the CAR warehouse. Open MWS410 to find the delivery and review its status.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MWS410 and filter for the delivery
  1. Open MWS410 (Delivery. Open Toolbox) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR (Carrollton) and click Apply.
  3. The list shows deliveries with columns: Dely no, Consignee, Dmt (Delivery method), Order no, and status.
  4. Locate the delivery for consignee 007822 - note the Delivery number and status (e.g., 1-Picking List Generated).
2. Review the delivery detail
  1. Click the Delivery number link to open the delivery detail.
  2. Review the delivery lines - confirm item, quantity, and warehouse location.
  3. Note the Delivery method (Dmt) field - OT means Our Truck, WC means Will Call.
  4. If status is 1-Picking List Generated, the pick list has been printed and picking is in progress.
3. Confirm pick status in MWS420
  1. Open MWS420 (Picking List. Report) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR, Pick sts = 40-Picking, and click Apply.
  3. Locate the delivery in the list - confirm it shows the correct print date (Prt dt) and pick time.
  4. Once warehouse confirms picking is complete, the delivery status will advance to the next step.
Expected Outcomes
  • Delivery located in MWS410 for warehouse CAR.
  • Pick list status confirmed in MWS420.
  • Delivery detail reviewed with correct items and quantities.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find a will call order in MWS420 and view the pick list
  • I can confirm pick quantities and adjust for shorts before saving
  • I can print the packing list for the customer
  • I can update the order status to ready for pickup and verify it in OIS100
Knowledge Check
1. Will call orders are found for picking in:
  • OIS100
  • MWS410
  • MWS420 (correct)
  • PPS300
2. The packing list given to the customer at pickup is printed from:
  • Birst
  • OIS100 directly
  • The print function in the picking program (correct)
  • PPS310
3. After confirming pick quantities and updating status, the order in OIS100 shows:
  • Status unchanged
  • An advanced status showing ready for pickup (correct)
  • A credit hold
  • A backorder
Session 2 - Logistics / Transportation

Common Carrier Shipping — Order to Shipment

45 min
Objective

Process a customer order shipment from pick list to delivery confirmation in M3.

Scenario

An OT (Our Truck) delivery for consignee 007822 has been picked. Confirm the delivery in MWS410 to advance the order to shipped status.

Practice Tasks
1. Verify the order and delivery in OIS300
  1. Open OIS300 (Customer Order. Open) and search for the order number (e.g., 1000000002).
  2. Confirm the order status - it should be at status 44 (Picking list printed) or higher.
  3. Verify the delivery address, carrier (Dmt = OT for Our Truck), and requested delivery date.
  4. Confirm all order lines are included and quantities match what was ordered.
2. Confirm the delivery in MWS410
  1. Open MWS410 (Delivery. Open Toolbox), set Warehouse = CAR, click Apply.
  2. Locate the delivery for order 1000000002.
  3. Right-click the delivery and look for a Confirm or Report option.
  4. Enter the actual shipped quantity if different from the ordered quantity.
  5. Confirm the delivery - M3 advances the order to shipped status.
3. Verify the order closes in OIS300
  1. Return to OIS300 and look up the order.
  2. Confirm the order status has advanced to 70 (Delivered) or higher.
  3. The invoice will be generated automatically based on the delivery confirmation.
  4. Verify the consignee 007822 account shows the new invoice in AR.
Expected Outcomes
  • Order status verified in OIS300 before shipment.
  • Delivery confirmed in MWS410 for warehouse CAR.
  • Order advanced to delivered status after confirmation.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can verify and update the carrier assignment in OIS100
  • I can generate a BOL in DPS100 with correct shipper, consignee, and freight class
  • I can print a customer manifest for today's route
  • I can enter a carrier tracking number back into the order after pickup
Knowledge Check
1. The carrier assigned to an order is shown in the Ship Via field in:
  • PPS200
  • MWS420
  • OIS100 (correct)
  • GLS040
2. A Bill of Lading for an LTL shipment is generated in:
  • OIS100
  • DPS100 (correct)
  • ARS120
  • PPS310
3. The customer manifest showing today's deliveries and COD amounts is found in:
  • Birst
  • WAR 8 > C3 (correct)
  • OIS300
  • MWS420
Session 3 - Logistics / Transportation

Carrier Setup and Freight Cost Management — MWS420

45 min
Objective

Print and review a pick list report in MWS420 for the CAR warehouse.

Scenario

The warehouse team needs the pick list for today's deliveries. Run MWS420 to print or display the picking list for CAR warehouse.

Practice Tasks
1. Open MWS420 and set filters
  1. Open MWS420 (Picking List. Report) from the left sidebar.
  2. Set Warehouse = CAR.
  3. Set Pick sts = 40-Picking to show orders actively being picked.
  4. Set Rep date = today's date.
  5. Click Apply - the list shows all deliveries with their delivery number, size (Sz), print date (Prt dt), and print time (Prt t).
2. Review the pick list
  1. The list shows columns: Dely no (delivery number), Sfx, Sz (size/carton count), Prt dt (print date), Prt t (print time), Pck t (pick time), Picker.
  2. Confirm the deliveries that need to go out today are listed.
  3. If a delivery is missing from the list, return to MWS410 and verify the delivery status.
  4. Click a delivery number to drill into the individual pick lines for that delivery.
3. Assign pickers and track progress
  1. Use the Picker field to assign a warehouse employee to a delivery if required.
  2. Once items are picked, the warehouse team enters the pick time (Pck t) to confirm completion.
  3. Deliveries with a pick time confirmed are ready for loading and shipment.
  4. Return to MWS410 to process the delivery confirmation once all lines are picked.
Expected Outcomes
  • Pick list report run in MWS420 for CAR warehouse.
  • Deliveries at pick status 40 reviewed.
  • Pick assignments tracked and confirmed.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can set up a new carrier record in MWS420 with correct ID, name, service type, and status
  • I check for duplicate carrier records before creating a new one
  • I understand the three freight term types — Prepaid, Collect, and Third Party
  • I can identify freight cost variances in OIS600 and know when to escalate
  • I track carrier performance — on-time, damage, tracking accuracy — and document patterns
  • I inactivate unused carriers rather than deleting them to preserve history
Knowledge Check
1. Before adding a new carrier in MWS420, you should first:
  • Call the carrier to confirm they can use M3
  • Search for the carrier name to confirm it does not already exist under a different ID (correct)
  • Get approval from Finance
  • Create a test delivery
2. Freight terms Collect means:
  • Adleta pays freight and bills the customer
  • The customer pays the carrier directly (correct)
  • A third-party account is billed
  • Freight is included in the product price
3. If a carrier consistently misses pickups or has high damage rates, you should:
  • Immediately delete the carrier from M3
  • Continue using them until a better option is found
  • Document the pattern and raise it with management for carrier review (correct)
  • Only use them for low-priority shipments
4. When a carrier is no longer used, you should:
  • Delete the carrier record
  • Inactivate the carrier — do not delete, to preserve freight history (correct)
  • Leave it active but stop using it
  • Archive it to SharePoint
Session 4 - Logistics / Transportation

Will Call — Pick, Pack, and Issue

1.5 hours
Objective

Look up shipment and delivery information using OIS600 for a customer order.

Scenario

A customer calls asking about delivery status for their order. Use OIS600 to look up the delivery and shipment details.

Practice Tasks
1. Open OIS600 and search for the delivery
  1. Open OIS600 (Delivery. Open) from the left sidebar.
  2. Filter by customer (consignee) number or order number to locate the delivery.
  3. Set Warehouse = CAR if searching for Carrollton shipments.
  4. Click Apply to see the matching deliveries.
2. Review delivery status and details
  1. OIS600 shows the delivery status, ship date, carrier (Dmt), and tracking information.
  2. Click the delivery to open the detail view and review individual lines.
  3. Confirm the shipped quantity and the actual delivery date.
  4. If the delivery shows OT (Our Truck), contact the driver or dispatch for an ETA.
3. Communicate status to the customer
  1. Note the delivery number and expected delivery date to share with the customer.
  2. If the delivery is confirmed (status 70+), advise the customer the order has shipped.
  3. If the delivery is still in picking (status 40-50), advise the customer of the expected ship date.
  4. Document the customer contact in the order's Your ref 1 field in OIS300 if needed.
Expected Outcomes
  • Delivery status looked up in OIS600.
  • Shipped quantity and date confirmed.
  • Customer advised of delivery status.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can locate and print a Will Call pick list in M3
  • I can confirm pick quantities in M3 after picking
  • I can issue a Will Call delivery at the delivery level
  • I understand Will Call does not require load building or dock assignment
  • I know how to stage orders for customer pickup
Knowledge Check
1. Will Call orders are issued at which level in M3?
  • Shipment level
  • Load level
  • Delivery level (correct)
  • Invoice level
2. Why do Will Call orders not require load building?
  • Will Call orders are too small for load planning
  • The customer picks up at the warehouse — no truck loading needed (correct)
  • IT disabled load building for Will Call
  • The warehouse team skips this step
3. When is inventory decremented for a Will Call order?
  • When the pick list is printed
  • When the customer confirms by phone
  • When the delivery is issued in M3 (correct)
  • At month-end inventory close
4. After issuing the Will Call delivery, the next step is:
  • Create a new order for the customer
  • Process an AP invoice
  • The order cycle is complete — inventory is decremented (correct)
  • Submit for supervisor approval
Session 5 - Logistics / Transportation

Common Carrier — Shipment Processing

1.5 hours
Objective

Understand the full logistics flow from order entry to delivery confirmation in M3.

Scenario

Walk through the end-to-end logistics process: order in OIS300, pick list in MWS410/MWS420, delivery confirmation, and how it connects to invoicing.

Practice Tasks
1. Order entry and release (OIS300)
  1. A customer order is entered in OIS300 (Customer Order. Open).
  2. Once the order is saved and released, M3 creates a delivery record automatically in MWS410.
  3. The order status advances from 11 (New) through to 44 (Picking list) as the logistics steps are completed.
  4. The delivery method (Dmt) on the order determines how it is handled - OT = Our Truck, WC = Will Call.
2. Pick list and picking (MWS410 and MWS420)
  1. Open MWS410 (Delivery. Open Toolbox) to see the delivery created for the order.
  2. The pick list is printed or displayed using MWS420 (Picking List. Report) - filter Warehouse = CAR, Pick sts = 40-Picking.
  3. Warehouse staff pick the items from location CAR1A10 (or the assigned location) and confirm quantities.
  4. Once picking is complete, the pick time is recorded and the delivery is ready to confirm.
3. Delivery confirmation and invoicing
  1. Return to MWS410 and confirm the delivery - M3 advances the order to status 70 (Delivered).
  2. Use OIS600 (Delivery. Open) to verify the delivery status and ship date after confirmation.
  3. M3 automatically generates the customer invoice based on the confirmed delivery.
  4. The invoice appears in AR for consignee 007822 and the order closes to status 90.
Expected Outcomes
  • Full logistics flow understood: OIS300 > MWS410 > MWS420 > OIS600.
  • Order status progression from 11 to 90 confirmed.
  • Delivery confirmation triggers automatic invoicing in AR.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can process a Common Carrier pick and pack workflow
  • I can issue a Common Carrier delivery at the delivery level in M3
  • I can enter a tracking number at the delivery level
  • I know how to update a delivery address before issuing
  • I understand that issuing the delivery triggers the AR invoice
Knowledge Check
1. Common Carrier deliveries are issued at the:
  • Shipment level
  • Delivery level (correct)
  • Load level
  • Order header level
2. Tracking numbers for Common Carrier shipments are entered in M3 at the:
  • Order header
  • Invoice
  • Pick list
  • Delivery level (correct)
3. A delivery address change must be completed:
  • After issuing the delivery
  • At any time up to invoicing
  • Before issuing the delivery (correct)
  • Only by a Finance user
4. After a Common Carrier delivery is issued in M3, what happens automatically?
  • A PO is created
  • The AR invoice is triggered (correct)
  • The item is back-ordered
  • The pick list is reprinted
