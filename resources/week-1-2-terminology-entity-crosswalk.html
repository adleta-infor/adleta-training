Session 1 - Foundation — Navigation and Core Concepts

Kickoff — Why M3, What to Expect, and Your Role

2 hours
Objective

Understand why Adleta is implementing M3 and what your super user role requires.

Scenario

You are attending the program kickoff. Review the go-live timeline, identify your module responsibilities, and locate your training resources on the portal.

Practice Tasks
1. Review the go-live timeline
  1. Open the portal and navigate to the Roadmap tab.
  2. Locate your module in the 9-week schedule.
  3. Note your week, owner name, and the programs listed for your module.
2. Understand your super user responsibilities
  1. On the Super Users tab, find your name and confirm your department.
  2. Review the three-phase model: Kristen trains you, you get certified, you train your department.
  3. Write down 2-3 team members you will be responsible for training.
3. Locate your training materials
  1. Navigate to your module on the Modules tab.
  2. Open the Guide link and confirm you can access the SharePoint document.
  3. Return to the portal and open Session 1 to review the checklist.
Expected Outcomes
  • You can locate your module and sessions on the portal.
  • You understand your role as the department trainer after certification.
  • You have access to your SharePoint learning guide.
Exception Handling
The portal will not load or shows an error
Clear your browser cache and try again. If the issue continues, notify Kristen - do not share your login with others to work around it.
You cannot find your name on the Super Users tab
Confirm your username with Kristen. Logins are case-sensitive.
Your SharePoint guide link shows Access Denied
You need to be added to the SharePoint site. Contact Kristen or IT - do not download and share the file directly.
A team member asks why they are not on the super user list
Only the designated super users per module are on the list. Redirect them to their department super user for training after certification.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can name my module and what I own
  • I understand my responsibilities before, during, and after go-live
  • I know the training model and what is required for sign-off
  • I have documented my biggest workflow concern in the issue log
Knowledge Check
1. Super users are responsible for supporting their department during:
  • Discovery workshops only
  • Go-live and the first 90 days (correct)
  • IT testing only
  • Annual audits
2. The Adleta training model ends with:
  • A written test
  • A teach-back to the group (correct)
  • A manager sign-off form
  • A Birst dashboard
3. Module ownership means a super user is accountable for:
  • Entering all data in their module
  • Supporting their department and escalating issues (correct)
  • Approving all transactions
  • Writing training documentation
Session 2 - Foundation — Navigation and Core Concepts

Getting Around M3 — The 6 Things You Need to Know

2 hours
Objective

Navigate M3 confidently using the 6 core navigation skills every super user must know.

Scenario

You are in the M3 training environment. Practice each navigation skill before your first module session.

Practice Tasks
1. Launch M3 and use the program search
  1. Log in to the M3 test environment with your training credentials.
  2. Type PPS200 in the program search bar and press Enter.
  3. Confirm the Purchase Order. Open panel loads.
2. Use function keys and panel navigation
  1. In PPS200, press F1 to access Help and review field descriptions.
  2. Press Escape to go back without saving.
  3. Use the breadcrumb at the top to confirm your location in M3.
3. Sort, filter, and right-click
  1. Open MMS200 (Item. Open) and type A in the Item Number field.
  2. Press Enter to display items beginning with A.
  3. Right-click any row and review the available options (Related, Tools).
  4. Click the column header to sort results ascending, then descending.
4. Open a related program
  1. From MMS200, right-click an item and select Related.
  2. Choose Stock Balance (MMS060) to view inventory for that item.
  3. Confirm the stock balance panel opens without re-entering the item number.
Expected Outcomes
  • You can launch any M3 program by typing its code in the search bar.
  • You can navigate between panels, sort lists, and right-click to related programs.
  • You understand the difference between Escape (cancel) and closing a panel.
Exception Handling
M3 opens but the program search bar is missing
You may be in a different view. Press F5 to refresh or look for the Search icon in the top navigation bar.
Pressing Enter in a search returns no results
Check for leading spaces or extra characters in the field. M3 searches are exact-match on the characters you type.
Right-click shows no Related option
The Related option only appears on list panels, not header panels. Make sure you are right-clicking a row in a list, not a field.
A panel opens read-only and you cannot change anything
Your user role may not have edit access for that program. Note the program name and report it to Kristen or IT.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open any M3 program by typing its code in the search bar
  • I can navigate panel tabs and name what each contains
  • I use right-click (not double-click) to Select, Change, and Display records
  • I can use the lookup icon to search for a customer, supplier, or item
  • I have submitted a test entry to the issue log
Knowledge Check
1. To open a program in M3, you type the program code in:
  • The URL bar
  • The left panel search bar (correct)
  • The Settings menu
  • The Help menu
2. In a list view, right-clicking a row and choosing Change does:
  • Deletes the record
  • Opens the record for editing (correct)
  • Displays the record read-only
  • Copies the record
3. The panel tabs at the bottom of a program (E, F, G...) represent:
  • Different companies
  • Different users logged in
  • Different data panels for the same record (correct)
  • Different warehouses
Session 3 - Foundation — Navigation and Core Concepts

Terminology — What We Call It vs. What M3 Calls It

1.5 hours
Objective

Map Adleta business terms to their M3 equivalents so you can find the right data in the right place.

Scenario

A team member asks where to find a customer account in M3. Practice looking up the key terminology mappings and locating each one in the system.

Practice Tasks
1. Look up customers in M3
  1. Open OIS300 (Customer. Open) in M3.
  2. Search for customer number 000382.
  3. Confirm the record shows - this is what Adleta calls a customer account.
2. Look up suppliers/suppliers
  1. Open CRS620 (Supplier. Open) in M3.
  2. Search for supplier ADL-V001.
  3. Note the M3 term is Supplier - Adleta calls this a vendor.
3. Locate a warehouse and item
  1. Open MMS060 (Item. Open) and search for any flooring item.
  2. Right-click and select Stock Balance to view warehouse 100.
  3. Confirm warehouse 100 is the main Adleta facility.
4. Self-quiz the terminology crosswalk
  1. Open the Foundation Learning Guide from the module Resources section.
  2. Find the Terminology Crosswalk table.
  3. Cover the M3 column and name the M3 term for each Adleta term without looking.
Expected Outcomes
  • You can find customers, suppliers, warehouses, and items in M3.
  • You know the key Adleta-to-M3 term mappings.
  • You can locate the correct program for any common lookup.
Exception Handling
You search for 000382 and get no results
Confirm you are in the correct program (OIS300 for customers, CRS620 for suppliers). Customer and supplier numbers live in different programs.
The M3 term does not match the Adleta term you expected
Use the Terminology Crosswalk in the Foundation Learning Guide as your reference. If something is missing, flag it for Kristen to add.
A team member uses the wrong program and enters data in the wrong place
Caught early: delete the incorrect entry and re-enter in the correct program. Caught after posting: escalate to the module owner - do not attempt to fix it yourself.
Warehouse 500 does not appear in stock balance
Warehouse 500 may not carry that item. An empty result is correct - it means no stock, not an error.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can name the M3 program for each core entity in my module
  • I can translate Adleta terms to M3 terms without notes
  • I can trace Procure to Pay from PO through supplier payment
  • I can trace Order to Cash from customer order through cash receipt
Knowledge Check
1. The M3 program for managing customer master records is:
  • PPS200
  • CRS610 (correct)
  • MMS060
  • OIS100
2. In Adleta terminology, a branch maps to which M3 entity?
  • Division
  • Warehouse (correct)
  • Company
  • Cost Center
3. Procure to Pay starts with a PO in PPS200 and ends with:
  • A customer invoice in OIS300
  • A cash receipt in ARS110
  • A supplier payment in APS130 (correct)
  • A stock receipt in MMS060
Session 4 - Foundation — Navigation and Core Concepts

Foundation Check — Prove You Can Do It

1.5 hours
Objective

Demonstrate all foundation skills without assistance to confirm readiness for module training.

Scenario

Kristen will call out tasks one at a time. Complete each one in M3 without referring to notes. This is your certification check for the Foundation module.

Practice Tasks
1. Navigation check
  1. Open PPS200 without using the menu - type it in the search bar.
  2. Open MMS200 and sort the item list by description.
  3. Use right-click Related to open stock balance from MMS200.
2. Lookup check
  1. Find supplier ADL-V001 and confirm their payment terms.
  2. Find customer 000382 and confirm their credit limit.
  3. Look up any item and confirm its stock in warehouse 100.
3. Terminology check
  1. State the M3 term for: Purchase Order, Vendor, Warehouse, Item Master, Customer.
  2. State the M3 program for: entering a sales order, receiving goods, viewing stock.
  3. Explain the difference between Escape and closing a panel to the trainer.
Expected Outcomes
  • You passed the Foundation check and are cleared for module training.
  • You can navigate M3 and locate records without assistance.
  • Your trainer has signed off on your Foundation readiness checklist.
Exception Handling
You cannot complete a task during the certification check
Tell Kristen immediately. Do not guess or skip - the certification check is pass/fail and you can reschedule.
You complete a task but Kristen marks it incorrect
Ask Kristen to walk through the correct steps with you before the next attempt. Take notes.
M3 is slow or unresponsive during the check
Notify Kristen. The check can be paused and rescheduled - a slow system is not a failure.
You pass navigation but struggle with terminology
You can be cleared for module training and revisit terminology as needed. Flag the gaps to Kristen so she can reinforce them in module sessions.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I opened three role-relevant programs without coaching
  • I looked up a customer, supplier, and item and named key fields
  • I right-clicked correctly to open a record for editing
  • I completed my teach-back without notes
  • All my open issues from fnd-1 through fnd-3 are assigned or resolved
Knowledge Check
1. During the navigation practical, you should open programs:
  • Using bookmarks a trainer set up
  • By typing program codes in the search bar without coaching (correct)
  • By asking IT to open them
  • From the help menu
2. To edit a record in a list view, the correct method is to:
  • Double-click the row
  • Single-click the row
  • Right-click and choose Change (correct)
  • Press F5
3. A super user is cleared for go-live when they:
  • Attend all sessions
  • Pass the teach-back and complete the issue log review (correct)
  • Complete the online quiz only
  • Get IT approval
