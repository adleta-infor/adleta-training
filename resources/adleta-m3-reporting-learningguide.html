Session 1 - Pricing and Reporting

Birst — Open Customer Order Book

40 min
Objective

Navigate to Infor M3 Analytics (Birst) and explore the Sales collection dashboards.

Scenario

Jeanell needs to check the open order book for the week. Open Birst from the M3 toolbar, navigate to the Sales collection, and review the Open Customer Order Book dashboard.

Practice Tasks
1. Open Birst from the M3 toolbar
  1. In M3, look at the top navigation bar - you will see tabs: Rhythm Commerce Business Center, Rhythm Portal, Infor M3, and Birst.
  2. Click the Birst tab. Infor M3 Analytics for M3 Cloud opens (version 2025.10.00).
  3. The landing page shows admin options and a Spaces list. You will work in the M3ADIS-Consumer space.
  4. Click into the M3ADIS-Consumer space if it does not open automatically.
2. Navigate to the Sales collection
  1. Click the hamburger menu (three lines) on the upper left to open the Collections panel.
  2. The Collections list shows: Home, Admin, Sales, Procurement, Finance, Production, Rental, Equipment, Warehouse, C-Suite.
  3. Click Sales. The first dashboard that opens is Open Customer Order Book.
  4. Note the breadcrumb at the top: Sales / Open Customer Order Book.
3. Review the Open Customer Order Book dashboard
  1. The filter chips at the top show: = 100 - CMP100 (company/division), = Open Orders, = Week, = [current week number], = 2026.
  2. Review the KPI tiles on the left: Overdue Line Amt, Line Amt, Order Value, No. of Stopped Order Lines, Order Qty.
  3. The charts on the right show Overdue Stock Analysis and Stop Code Analysis by week.
  4. To change the week, click the week number filter chip and select a different period.
  5. To see all Sales dashboards, click the dropdown arrow next to Open Customer Order Book in the breadcrumb - there are 17 Sales dashboards total.
Expected Outcomes
  • Birst opens from the M3 toolbar - no separate login needed.
  • Sales collection shows 17 dashboards including Open Customer Order Book.
  • Open Customer Order Book shows current week open orders, overdue amounts, and stopped order lines.
Exception Handling
The Birst tab is not visible in the M3 toolbar
The Birst tab appears next to the Infor M3 tab in the top navigation bar. If it is not there, try refreshing M3 or contact IT - your user account may not have Birst access yet.
Birst opens but shows no data for the current week
Birst data loads daily at approximately 6:02 AM. If today's ETL has not run yet, data will reflect yesterday. Check Admin > Incremental Load Logs to confirm the last successful load date.
The dashboard shows data but the numbers look unusually high or low
Check the Company filter - it should show 100 - CMP100. If it is blank or set to a different company, the numbers will be wrong. Click the filter chip and set it to 100.
You cannot find the Sales collection
Click the hamburger menu on the upper left. If it is already open and you do not see Sales, scroll down in the Collections panel - it appears after Admin.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open Birst and the Sales collection
  • I can read the Open Customer Order Book tiles and filters
  • I use OIS300 for live order status
Knowledge Check

  • The Birst tab in M3's top bar (correct)
  • A separate website with its own login
  • OIS300
  • Teams
2. The first Sales dashboard is:
  • Open Customer Order Book (correct)
  • Sales Manager - Margin
  • AP Aging
  • Stock Count
3. For one order's live status, use:
  • OIS300 in M3 (correct)
  • Birst
  • Excel
  • GLS990
Session 2 - Pricing and Reporting

Birst — Sales Manager Margin

40 min
Objective

Use the Sales Manager - Margin dashboard in Birst to review gross margin performance.

Scenario

Jeanell needs to review margin performance by product and customer for the current year. Open the Sales Manager - Margin dashboard and identify any margin concerns.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the Sales Manager - Margin dashboard
  1. In Birst with the Sales collection open, click the dropdown arrow next to the current dashboard name in the breadcrumb.
  2. Select Sales Manager - Margin from the list.
  3. The dashboard header shows: Sales Manager - Margin | Currency: USD | Year: 2026.
2. Review the KPI tiles and charts
  1. The four KPI tiles across the top show: Gross Margin % FY, Invcd Net Amt, Gross Profit $, COGS Standard.
  2. Gross Margin % FY is the primary metric - this is your year-to-date margin percentage.
  3. Below the tiles are charts: Sales Margin by Period (trend over time), Pct of Total by Product, Pct of Total by Customer, Invcd Amt Breakdown of Top Gross Profit 2, and Top Sales Margin.
  4. Sales Margin by Period shows how margin has trended week by week or month by month.
3. Filter and drill into margin details
  1. The filter chips show: = 100 - CMP100, = Invcd Net Amt, = [period], = 2026.
  2. To change from weekly to monthly view, click the period filter chip (Week/Month) and switch to Month.
  3. To drill into a specific product line, click Drill Across on the Pct of Total by Product chart.
  4. Note any products or customers showing lower-than-expected margin for follow-up.
Expected Outcomes
  • Gross Margin % FY, Invcd Net Amt, Gross Profit $, and COGS Standard reviewed.
  • Sales Margin by Period chart shows margin trend across the year.
  • Product and customer margin breakdowns visible for drill-down.
Exception Handling
All KPI tiles show 0 or No data was returned
The filter period may have no invoiced data. Change the period filter from Week to Month, or change the year. In the test environment, data may only exist for certain periods.
Gross Margin % FY seems much lower than expected
Confirm the filter is set to Invcd Net Amt (invoiced amount) and not a different measure. Also confirm the company filter = 100 - CMP100. If both are correct, bring the number to Tony Shipka before taking action.
You want to export the margin data to Excel
Use the export icon in the dashboard toolbar (the share/upload icon). Select the export format. If the export is blocked by a popup blocker, allow popups for the Birst URL and try again.
A product line is missing from the Pct of Total by Product chart
The chart may only show top N products. Use the Sales Analysis Ad Hoc dashboard or drill across to see the full product list including items with small volumes.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can open Sales Manager - Margin
  • I can read the four KPI tiles
  • I can change period and drill across
Knowledge Check
1. Year-to-date margin % is the tile:
  • Gross Margin % FY (correct)
  • Order Qty
  • Line Amt
  • Stop Code
2. To dig into one product line, use:
  • Drill Across (correct)
  • Delete
  • Export only
  • Refresh
3. Margin trend over time is in:
  • Sales Margin by Period (correct)
  • Stop Code Analysis
  • Overdue Stock
  • AP Aging
Session 3 - Pricing and Reporting

Birst — Customer Profitability and Ad Hoc Reports

45 min
Objective

Use the Customer Profitability Analysis dashboard in Birst to review revenue and margin by customer. Then pull a custom report from Sales Analysis Ad Hoc.

Scenario

Jeanell needs to identify which customers are driving the most profit this year. Open Customer Profitability Analysis and review the top customers.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the Customer Profitability Analysis dashboard
  1. In Birst Sales collection, click the dashboard dropdown in the breadcrumb and select Customer Profitability Analysis.
  2. The dashboard loads showing customer-level revenue and profitability data.
  3. Confirm the filters show Company = 100 - CMP100 and Year = 2026.
2. Review customer profitability
  1. Review the top customers by invoiced amount and gross profit.
  2. Look for customers with high revenue but low margin - these may need pricing review.
  3. Look for customers with declining revenue year-over-year - these may need sales follow-up.
  4. Use the Drill Across option on any chart to get more detail on a specific customer.
3. Connect findings to M3 for action
  1. If a customer shows an unusual margin, open M3 and check their price list in OIS017 (price lists).
  2. Search by customer number - remember the format is six digits, e.g., 000382 for AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES.
  3. Compare the OIS017 price list entries to the Birst margin data to identify whether pricing or cost is the issue.
  4. Document any customers needing pricing review and bring to Tony Shipka.
4. Set the filters for the report
  1. Set Company = 100 - CMP100.
  2. Set the date range to cover the last 3 months.
  3. If available, filter by the relevant product category or item group.
  4. Click Apply or refresh to run the report.
5. Review and export the results
  1. Review the data returned - confirm it matches the scope of the request (correct dates, correct division).
  2. Use the export function to download to Excel.
  3. Name the file clearly with the date range and criteria (e.g., Sales_by_ProductGroup_Q2_2026).
  4. Share the export with the sales manager - do not share the Birst link directly unless they have Birst access.
Expected Outcomes
  • Top customers by revenue and profit identified in Birst.
  • Customers with margin concerns flagged for pricing review.
  • Connection made between Birst profitability data and M3 price list in OIS017.
  • Sales by product category pulled for the last 3 months from Birst.
  • Data exported to Excel and ready to share.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find margin problems in Customer Profitability Analysis
  • I can check prices in OIS017
  • I can build and export an ad hoc report
Knowledge Check
1. Low margin on a big customer suggests:
  • A pricing review (correct)
  • Nothing
  • A credit hold
  • A new route
2. For a custom date range and filters, use:
  • Sales Analysis Ad Hoc (correct)
  • Open Customer Order Book
  • OIS300
  • GLS910
3. Someone without Birst access needs the data. Send:
  • The Excel export (correct)
  • The Birst link
  • Your password
  • Nothing

Customer Prices — OIS017

45 min
Objective

Check and update a customer-specific price in OIS017 and verify it applies to a new order.

Scenario

Customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) has a negotiated price for a specific item that differs from the standard list. Verify and update it in OIS017.

Practice Tasks
1. Open OIS017 and look up the customer
  1. Press Ctrl+R, type OIS017, and open price lists.
  2. In the filter fields, enter Customer = 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES).
  3. Press Enter or click Apply to load the customer-specific price list.
  4. OIS017 shows only items with negotiated prices for this customer - not the full catalog.
2. Review the customer price entries
  1. Each line shows: item number, item name, customer-specific price, unit, and effective/expiration dates.
  2. Confirm each price matches the current negotiated agreement with Alan Wheelock.
  3. Note: a customer-specific price in OIS017 always overrides the standard price list in the standard price list for that customer.
  4. If an item is not listed in OIS017, the customer will be charged the standard the standard price list price.
3. Update or add a customer price
  1. To update an existing price: select the line and click Change. Enter the new price and effective date.
  2. To add a new item: click the + (New) button. Enter the customer number, item number, price, and effective date.
  3. Save and verify the entry appears in the list.
  4. To confirm the price will apply to a new order, open OIS100 and start a test order for customer 000382 with the item - the price should auto-fill from OIS017.
Expected Outcomes
  • Customer 000382 price verified in OIS017.
  • Customer-specific price updated with correct amount and effective date.
  • OIS017 price confirmed to override the standard price list standard price for this customer.
Exception Handling
OIS017 shows no entries for customer 000382
This customer may be using only the standard the standard price list price list with no negotiated exceptions. That is normal. If the customer should have a special price, add it using the + button.
You updated the price in OIS017 but a new order still shows the old price
Check the effective date - if it is set to a future date, the new price will not apply until that date. Also confirm the item number in OIS017 exactly matches the item on the order.
The customer number you enter in OIS017 is rejected
Customer numbers in M3 are six digits with leading zeros: 000382, not 000382. If you are unsure of a customer number, look it up in CRS610 first - the Customer column shows the correct format.
A customer asks why their price changed on a recent order
Check the effective date on their OIS017 entry. If a new price went live and was applied to an order the customer expected the old price on, bring the details to Tony Shipka and Philip Jones before making any adjustment.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I know price lists are in OIS017, not OIS010
  • I can read and update a price line with dates
  • I test a price change on a test order
Knowledge Check
1. Customer price lists are maintained in:
  • OIS017 (correct)
  • OIS010 (that's CO types)
  • CRS620
  • MMS310
2. A promotional price should have:
  • A valid-to date (correct)
  • No dates
  • A new item number
  • A credit hold
3. The price on the order is wrong. First check:
  • Overlapping date ranges in OIS017 (correct)
  • GLS200
  • MWS410
  • PPS250

Price List Maintenance and Price Increases — OIS017

40 min
Objective

Review and update a customer price list in OIS017 in M3.

Scenario

A price update is needed for a product line. Review the current price list in OIS017 and update the effective price and date.

Practice Tasks
1. Open OIS017 and find the price list
  1. Open OIS017 (price lists) with Ctrl+R.
  2. OIS017 shows the standard price lists used across all customers.
  3. Use the filter fields to search by price list ID or item group.
  4. Locate the price list that applies to the product line needing an update.
2. Review the price list entries
  1. Click into the price list to view the line-level entries.
  2. Each line shows: item number, item name, price, unit of measure, and effective dates.
  3. Confirm the current price matches what is in the supplier agreement or product management direction.
  4. Note the expiration date - if it has passed, the price will not apply to new orders.
3. Update the price
  1. Select the price list line to update and click Change.
  2. Enter the new price and the effective date (the date the new price should start applying to orders).
  3. If the old price should stop applying before the new price starts, also update the expiration date on the old line.
  4. Save the change and confirm the new price appears correctly in the list.
Expected Outcomes
  • Price list entry updated in OIS017 with new price and effective date.
  • New orders created after the effective date will use the updated price.
  • Old price line has correct expiration date so there is no overlap.
Exception Handling
The price list you need is not in OIS017
OIS017 shows standard price lists. Customer-specific prices are in OIS017. If the price list does not appear in OIS017, check OIS017 with the customer number (format: 000382).
The new price takes effect but existing open orders still show the old price
M3 prices orders at the time of entry. An open order keeps its original price unless manually updated. If a customer was quoted the new price, update the order line manually with Tony Shipka approval.
You are not sure which effective date to use
The effective date should be the date you and Tony Shipka agreed the new price applies to new orders. Do not backdate a price change without explicit approval from Philip Jones.
The price list line shows a unit of measure you do not recognize
Common units in M3: CT = cartons, EA = each, SF = square feet, LF = linear feet, PC = pieces. If an unfamiliar unit appears, confirm with Lauren Calvey before changing the price - the unit affects the total calculation.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I check the customer's assigned price list
  • I snapshot, change with dates, and compare
  • I tell Sales before the effective date
Knowledge Check
1. Before a price increase, first:
  • Export the current list as a snapshot (correct)
  • Delete the old prices
  • Close the period
  • Change OIS010
2. To avoid overlap, the old price line should:
  • End the day before the new one starts (correct)
  • Be deleted
  • Stay open
  • Be copied
3. OIS010 at Adleta is:
  • CO Type. Open (order types) (correct)
  • Price lists
  • Customer master
  • Supplier master

Price Overrides on Customer Orders

35 min
Objective

Change the price on one order line and document why.

Scenario

Customer 000382 was quoted a lower price on ARM51830031 than the price list. Your manager has approved it for this order only.

Practice Tasks
1. Start the order
  1. Press Ctrl+R, open OIS100, find 000382, and click Next through to OIS101.
  2. Enter item ARM51830031 and quantity. Note the price that fills in.
2. Override the price
  1. Change the sales price on the line to the approved price.
  2. Read any warning M3 shows and write it down.
  3. Click Add.
3. Document
  1. Add a note on the order: approved price, who approved, why.
  2. If this customer will get this price on every order, ask for the price list (OIS017) to be updated instead.
Expected Outcomes
  • Line entered at the approved price.
  • Warning noted.
  • Approval documented on the order.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • Understands when a price override is and is not appropriate
  • Can locate and change the unit price on an OIS100 order line
  • Knows what to do if the override requires manager authorization
  • Documents every price override with a reason and authorization in order notes
  • Can find the price override report in Birst to monitor trends
  • Knows how to correct a pricing error before and after invoicing
Knowledge Check
1. When is a price override on an order line appropriate?
  • Any time a customer asks for a better price
  • For one-time situations like matching a competitor quote or correcting an error — not for routine use (correct)
  • Whenever the standard price seems too high
  • Only on will call orders
2. What should you always do after overriding a price on an order?
  • Email the customer the new price
  • Add a note to the order explaining why the price was changed and who authorized it (correct)
  • Delete the original price from OIS017
  • Call the warehouse to hold the order
3. What does M3 do when a price override exceeds your authorization limit?
  • It applies the override automatically
  • It sends an email to the customer
  • It places the order on hold and requires manager approval before it can be released (correct)
  • It cancels the order
4. If you find yourself overriding the same customer and item price every order, what should you do instead?
  • Keep overriding — it saves time
  • Update the price permanently in OIS017 so M3 calculates it correctly on every new order (correct)
  • Ask IT to disable the standard price for that item
  • Apply a blanket discount in the customer master
