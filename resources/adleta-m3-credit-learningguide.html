Session 1 - Credit and Accounts Receivable

Credit Holds — Reviewing and Releasing Orders

1 hour
Objective

Review and release a credit-held order.

Scenario

An order for customer 000382 is held for credit limit (CO stop 3). Review the account and release it.

Practice Tasks
1. Find the hold
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS300, and click OK.
  2. Use the credit view and find the held order for 000382.
2. Review the account
  1. Open CRS610 for 000382, panel J: note the credit limit and terms.
  2. Open CRS315 for 000382. Press F15 to refresh and compare limit to balance.
  3. Open ARS200 for 000382 and check the aging.
3. Release
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type OIS120, and click OK.
  2. Find the order and release it as your trainer directs (0 or 9).
  3. Check OIS300: the credit flag is off.
Expected Outcomes
  • Held order found in OIS300.
  • Account reviewed in CRS610, CRS315, ARS200.
  • Order released in OIS120.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find held orders in OIS300
  • I can read CRS610 J/K, CRS315, and ARS200
  • I can release an order in OIS120 with my trainer
  • I know prepay holds are not credit holds
Knowledge Check
1. Credit-held orders are released in:
  • OIS120 (correct)
  • OIS100
  • MWS410
  • APS100
2. Limit vs current balance (refresh with F15) is in:
  • CRS315 (correct)
  • CRS620
  • GLS200
  • PPS040
3. The credit manager for a customer is on:
  • CRS610 panel K (correct)
  • OIS101
  • MMS002
  • DRS011
Session 2 - Credit and Accounts Receivable

Setting a Customer to Prepay — CRS610 Panel J

45 min
Objective

Recognize and clear a prepay hold.

Scenario

A test customer is set to Payment terms PCD in company 100. Key a small C01 order and follow it until it can be picked.

Practice Tasks
1. Key the order
  1. Enter a small C01 order for a stocked item for the PCD test customer (your trainer provides it).
  2. Finish the order and note the CO no.
2. Find the hold
  1. In OIS300, confirm the order shows no credit flag.
  2. In MWS410, find the delivery. Look for stop C10 "delivery not paid".
3. Clear it
  1. Take the payment in OIS215 on cash desk 101A.
  2. Re-check MWS410: the C10 stop should be gone and the delivery can be released.
  3. Write down what you saw. This test also settles open points in Mike's prepay guide.
Expected Outcomes
  • C10 stop seen on a PCD order.
  • No credit flag in OIS300.
  • Stop cleared after payment in OIS215.
Exception Handling
No C10 stop appears
Note it and tell Mike. The order type or a prepayment selection table may also matter. This is one of the things the test is checking.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can set PCD on CRS610 panel J in company 100
  • I know a credit limit isn't needed for prepay
  • I can run the prepay test and record results
Knowledge Check
1. To make a customer prepay you change:
  • Their payment terms on CRS610 panel J (correct)
  • A prepay checkbox
  • The order type only
  • The warehouse
2. Which terms make orders wait for payment today?
  • PCD and CSH (correct)
  • COD and PP
  • N30
  • CH1
3. Where should you make the change first?
  • Company 100 (test) (correct)
  • Production
  • Either
  • Neither
Session 3 - Credit and Accounts Receivable

Customer Terms and Credit Setup — CRS610

45 min
Objective

Review and update AR settings for a customer including payment terms and credit limit in CRS610.

Scenario

Review the AR configuration for customer 000382 (AAA EXPRESS WATER AND FIRE RES) - confirm payment terms, credit limit, and payment method are set correctly.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the customer record in CRS610
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type CRS610, and open Customer. Open.
  2. Search for customer 000382.
  3. Go to panel J (the financial panel with the credit limits and payment terms).
2. Review AR payment settings
  1. Confirm Payment terms = N30 (30 days net).
  2. Confirm Payment mtd AR = CH1 (Chk,allc,no rem).
  3. Note the Credit group field - this controls credit limit checking.
  4. If payment terms need to be changed, click the edit icon and update the Payment terms field.
3. Save and verify
  1. Save any changes to the customer record.
  2. Create a test order for customer 000382 in OIS300 and confirm payment terms default to N30.
  3. Check that the CO stop field on the order is 0-No stop.
Expected Outcomes
  • Customer 000382 AR settings reviewed in CRS610.
  • Payment terms N30 and payment method CH1 confirmed.
  • Test order verified with correct defaults.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can find terms, limits, and stop on CRS610 panel J
  • I know the payment method is a default only
  • I know limit changes need approval
  • I know ship-tos are in OIS002
Knowledge Check
1. Credit limits and payment terms are on CRS610 panel:
  • E
  • J (correct)
  • B
  • H
2. The payment method on the customer:
  • Blocks checks
  • Is a default only (correct)
  • Sets the credit limit
  • Sets the route
3. Customer ship-to addresses are added in:
  • OIS002 (correct)
  • PPS040
  • MMS008
  • GLS040

The Customer Ledger — ARS200

45 min
Objective

Review a customer's balance before a credit decision.

Scenario

Credit is deciding whether to release an order for customer 000382. Pull the account picture.

Practice Tasks
1. Open the ledger
  1. Press Ctrl+S, type ARS200, and click OK.
  2. Find customer 000382.
2. Read it
  1. Note total open balance and the oldest open invoice.
  2. Note any unapplied payments or credits.
3. Compare to the limit
  1. Open CRS315 for 000382 and press F15.
  2. Compare the limit to the balance. Write a one-line summary for the credit decision.
Expected Outcomes
  • Open balance, oldest invoice, and unapplied items noted.
  • Limit vs balance compared.
  • Summary ready for the credit decision.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I can read a customer's ledger and aging in ARS200
  • I know the numbers before any credit call
  • I know where invoices and manual items live
Knowledge Check

  • OIS301
2. To see the invoice document, use:
  • OIS350 (correct)
  • ARS110
  • MMS001

  • ARS120 (correct)

Session 7 - Credit and Accounts Receivable

Collections Follow-Up

1 hour
Objective

Prepare and document a collections call.

Scenario

Customer 000382 has an invoice over 45 days past due.

Practice Tasks
1. Prepare
  1. Open ARS200 for 000382.
  2. Note the past-due invoice, amount, and total open.
2. Call and document
  1. Make the (practice) call.
  2. Record who you spoke to, the date, what was promised, and the next follow-up date where your trainer shows you.
3. Escalate if needed
  1. If this is the third missed promise, tell your manager. Credit decides on a stop in CRS610.
Expected Outcomes
  • Account reviewed before the call.
  • Contact documented with next follow-up date.
  • Escalation path followed.
Sign-Off Checklist
  • I review ARS200 before calling
  • I record every contact and promise
  • I escalate before any stop is set
Knowledge Check
1. Before a collections call, review:
  • ARS200 aging (correct)
  • PPS220
  • MWS410
  • GLS990
2. A customer stop is set:
  • By credit, with approval (correct)
  • By the CSR
  • By the warehouse
  • Automatically after 1 call
3. Every contact note should include:
  • Who, when, what was promised, next date (correct)
  • Only the amount
  • Nothing
  • The item number
